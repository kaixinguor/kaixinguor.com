11/24 20:34 新浪科技 ↗

小鹏发布图灵世界模型 WorldGPT：一个云端 teacher model 覆盖水陆空六类本体，先规划动作再预演世界

11 月 24 日晚，小鹏发布图灵世界模型家族的新成员 WorldGPT，定位为「世界动作模型」。在小鹏的物理 AI 体系里，它担任云端 teacher model：给定一段历史画面和一条指令，模型先规划动作，再预演世界随之发生的变化，并用语言完成推理与决策。

小鹏称，这是首个能在一套模型里覆盖水、陆、空三栖六类本体、完成预测与行动规划的世界模型。此前的行业做法是一机一栈——汽车一套系统、机械臂一套、无人机又是一套；本体越多成本越高，数据被切得越碎，而真正难的地方在于动作无法在机器之间直接共享。

WorldGPT 的解法是用容量更大的云端模型做跨本体训练，提取共有的世界知识与通用动作直觉，再针对每种本体蒸馏出小型本地模型，兼顾通用能力与端侧部署。小鹏称，过去支持一种新本体的边际成本约等于全量成本：采数据、训模型、调部署、整条链路重来；现在只需在基座上做特化，用少量本体数据校准控制细节。

小鹏称该模型在驾驶与机器人操作两大领域「展现出领先实力」，但没有公布具体基准、成功率或对比对象。

看点跨本体这件事，能验证的只有三个数：teacher 有多大、蒸馏出来的学生模型参数量与延迟是多少、六类本体上的失败模式是否一致。如果共享的只是视觉表征，那它更像一个更好的视觉骨干；如果连动作空间都对齐了，才谈得上「一套模型多机通用」。目前没有权重、没有 API、没有第三方基准，「首个」是厂商口径，不是可复现的结论。
另一个视角一机一栈变成一栈多机，摊薄的是每类本体的研发固定成本；但云端 teacher 的训练与数据采集仍是一次性的大额支出，能摊到多少台设备，决定这门生意最终是项目制还是平台制。

腾讯混元开源第三代翻译模型 Hy MT3：1.8B 起三个尺寸，先上端侧与自家翻译 App

11 月 24 日，腾讯混元发布并开源第三代翻译模型 Hy MT3，包含 1.8B、7B 与 30B-A3B（MoE）三个尺寸，面向端侧场景，覆盖 PC、手机与各类智能设备。

模型已在腾讯 Hy 翻译 App 和小程序上线，开源版本支持自行下载部署。据 腾讯新闻，混元方面称该模型支持 102 种语言。

三个尺寸的分工很清楚：1.8B 塞进手机与嵌入式设备，7B 覆盖 PC 与工作站，30B-A3B 用 MoE 把每 token 的激活参数压到 3B 级，在服务端换取质量。混元把翻译单独做成一条开源产品线，已经是第三代。

看点翻译是端侧最刚需、也最容易量化的任务：延迟以毫秒计、质量有 BLEU 与 COMET 这类可比指标、语种数量可以数。MoE 放在翻译上是务实的选择——翻译负载是短句、高并发，激活参数小意味着单卡能扛更多请求。接下来要看的数是量化后的实际内存占用、首 token 延迟，以及 102 种语言里有多少是「够用」而不是「支持」。

英国 NCSC：AI 辅助攻击自 9 月以来明显增加，红队框架被拿来打真实目标

11 月 24 日，英国国家网络安全中心（NCSC）的三位官员发文称，自 9 月以来观察到 AI 辅助的网络攻击「明显增加」，多个行业的组织都报告了由此引发的事件。

按文中描述，攻击者用的是「agentic offensive frameworks」——原本为红队演练设计的软件被用到防御之外——再叠加廉价的开源模型或非法转售的 API。攻击形态是快速、自动地发现漏洞，并在短时间内连续尝试目标全部联网资产；受害者的后果从服务中断到系统被控不等。

文章署名三人：负责架构的首席技术官 David Chismon、事件响应人员 Kris B.、漏洞管理团队首席数据科学家 Ellen T.。他们强调，这些漏洞与攻击手法都「不新」，绝大多数仍然可以修补，NCSC 看到的情况几乎都能靠基础安全实践、零信任与到位的技术控制避免。

同日，据 CyberScoop 报道，CrowdStrike 首席情报分析师 Eric Loui 举了一个阿根廷医疗机构的案例：攻击者只让模型「找一个入口」，既不指定服务也不教它侦察，90 分钟后 agent 已经定位并利用了一个 SQL 注入漏洞。他还提到，旧模型的护栏容易绕过，最坏情况下重开会话再试一次即可。

看点这一条最该记住的不是「AI 黑客来了」，而是成本结构变了：过去一次完整渗透要投入人力与时间，现在是一套 harness 加一个便宜模型，可以并行试完目标的所有资产。防守方可测的量因此回到基础项——暴露资产清点、密钥轮换、API 权限收敛、补丁闭环时间。口径也要注意：NCSC 说的是「事件增加」，没有给样本量与统计方法，别把它读成一张攻击数量曲线。

黎曼猜想证明两天被砍掉 240 万行：1737 万行 Lean 代码，只有六成被主证明真正用到

11 月 24 日，据量子位报道，Anthropic 数学家 Levent Alpöge 与 Claude 合作完成的黎曼猜想形式化证明，正在被全球开发者接力压缩。这份证明包含 1737 万行 Lean 4 代码、175187 个文件；Lean 与 Mathlib 社区此前已确认它通过了四道关卡的形式化验证。

压缩进展以仓库指标呈现：GitHub 官方镜像仓库 riemann-hypothesis 收到 359 个拉取请求，有效代码被砍掉 240 多万行，文件数减少 2.5 万多个，仓库累计已有 800 多次提交、近 300 个待处理请求。第三方追踪站 rh-shrink 显示，去掉空行、注释与引用声明后，有效代码从 1462 万行降到 1211 万行，减少 16.5%。

另一个站点 rh-kernel-backbone 给出了原因：1737.4 万行里，真正被主证明用到的只有 1073.7 万行，刚过六成；269.4 万行是主证明完全没用上的死代码，另有约 24.1 万条定理是重复写的。

压缩还被做成了公开打榜赛。VerifyTask 把占全证明 92.23% 的 158055 个文件拆成 39 个无假设核心模块，逐模块提交更短的等价 Lean 4 代码，每份提交都要通过 Lean 4.34.1、4.33.1 与 Comparator 的零 sorry 校验。截至报道时，榜单记录了 955 次通过校验的运行和 301 次有效突破，这批核心模块的标准化 token 总数减少 5048 万个，整体压缩率 8.4%。

有意思的是排名：写出原证明的 Claude Opus 5.5 配 Claude Code 只排第 7，拿下 8 个核心模块；第一位是开发者用多模型系统 The Council 配 pi 工具链，拿下 28 个模块、平均压缩率 66.6%；第二位是 Gemini 4 Neon 配 Antigravity，18 个模块、平均压缩率 66.4%，103 次提交全部一次通过；第三、第四位分别是 DeepSeek Flash V4.1 与 GPT-6.2 Astra。有开发者把四个模型和三个终端工具接成一条流水线，把 1 号模块从 21 个文件压成 1 个，token 减少 95.4%。

看点形式化证明的瓶颈已经从「对不对」变成「读不读得懂」。最值得记的不是删了多少行，而是那个比值：1737 万行里只有 1073 万行被主证明引用，说明模型在多处重复造引理——这正是自动生成代码的典型冗余。另一个可复用的结论是跨模型互查：原作者的模型自审排第 7，别家模型与混编流水线压得更狠，等于一次大规模实证「让结构不同的模型挑刺比自己回头看更有效」。口径要分清：8.4% 是 token 数，16.5% 是有效行数，两个数不能混着比。

XPENG unveils WorldGPT: one cloud teacher model covers six bodies on land, water and air, planning the action before rolling the world forward

On the evening of November 24, XPENG introduced WorldGPT, a "world-action model" and the newest member of its Turing world-model family. Within XPENG's physical-AI stack it acts as a cloud teacher model: given past frames and an instruction, it plans the action, predicts how the world will change as a result, and carries out the reasoning and decision in language.

XPENG says it is the first world model to cover six bodies across land, water and air in a single model, handling both prediction and action planning. The industry norm has been one stack per robot — one system for a car, another for an arm, a third for a drone. More body types mean higher cost and more fragmented data, and the hard part is that actions cannot be shared directly between machines.

WorldGPT's answer is to train a larger cloud model across bodies to extract shared world knowledge and general action intuition, then distil a small local model for each body, balancing general capability against on-device deployment. XPENG says supporting a new body used to cost roughly as much as starting from scratch — collect data, train, tune deployment, redo the whole chain — whereas now a new body only has to specialise on top of the base and calibrate control details with a little of its own data.

XPENG says the model "leads" in both driving and robot manipulation, but published no benchmark, success rate or comparison set.

看点Three numbers would settle the cross-body claim: how large the teacher is, the parameter count and latency of the distilled student, and whether failure modes are consistent across the six bodies. If what is shared is only a visual representation, this is a better vision backbone; only if the action space is aligned too is it "one model, many machines". Today there are no weights, no API and no third-party benchmark, so "first" is a vendor claim rather than a reproducible result.
另一个视角Going from one stack per robot to one stack for many spreads the fixed R&D cost per body type, but training the cloud teacher and collecting its data remain large one-off outlays. How many devices the cost can be spread across decides whether this ends up a project business or a platform one.

11/24 17:00 Jiemian News ↗

Tencent Hunyuan open-sources Hy MT3, a third-generation translation model starting at 1.8B and aimed at devices

On November 24, Tencent Hunyuan released and open-sourced Hy MT3, its third-generation translation model, in three sizes — 1.8B, 7B and 30B-A3B (a mixture-of-experts) — aimed at on-device scenarios spanning PCs, phones and smart devices.

The model is already live in Tencent's Hy Translate app and mini-program, and the open weights can be downloaded and deployed locally. Hunyuan says it covers 102 languages, according to Tencent News.

The division of labour between the three sizes is straightforward: 1.8B fits on a phone or an embedded device, 7B covers PCs and workstations, and 30B-A3B uses sparse activation to bring active parameters per token down to the 3B class in exchange for quality on the server side. Translation is now a standalone open-source product line for Hunyuan, three generations in.

看点Translation is the most obviously quantified on-device task: latency measured in milliseconds, quality comparable through BLEU and COMET, and a language count you can actually count. MoE is a pragmatic fit — translation traffic is short utterances at high concurrency, so a small active parameter count means more requests per card. The numbers to watch next are post-quantisation memory, time to first token, and how many of those 102 languages are "usable" rather than merely "supported".

Two days, 2.4 million lines gone: only about 60% of the 17.37-million-line Lean proof is actually used

According to a November 24 report by QbitAI, the formal proof of the Riemann hypothesis that Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge produced with Claude is being compressed by developers around the world. Compression has also become a public leaderboard. VerifyTask split the 158,055 files that make up 92.23% of the proof into 39 assumption-free core modules, inviting shorter equivalent Lean 4 code for each, with every submission checked against Lean 4.34.1, 4.33.1 and the Comparator with zero sorry. The reusable lesson is cross-model review — the author's own model ranks seventh while other models and mixed pipelines cut deeper, a large-scale demonstration that a differently structured model finds your dead weight better than you do. Keep the units apart: 8.4% is a token measure, 16.5% is effective lines.

看点This is the template for buying GPUs with bonds: the chip is both capital equipment and collateral, a five-year lease races the GPU's depreciation curve, and 8.26% is what the market charges for chip-obsolescence risk.
