01 模型与能力

谷歌开源 Harness²：不动权重，让智能体自己改自己的“外壳”

谷歌开源了框架 Harness²（github.com/google-research/harness2，论文 arXiv 2610.14224），思路是不改模型参数，而是让智能体自己改写包在模型外面的那层“马具”——系统提示词、护栏、记忆、工具指导、子智能体、技能、脚本、插件共八个可编辑组件。它做双层递归：任务级用“提议–探测–组合”挑出对当前任务最有效的改法，元级把成功的改法和执行轨迹沉淀下来，指导下一个陌生任务。

看点权重改不动的时候（闭源 API、算力贵、还会灾难性遗忘），“改外壳”几乎是唯一还能落地的路径。更值得记的是数字：单步优化后 WorkBuddy-Bench +11.5、JobBench +4.5、LAB +11.8。模型没变、成绩变了——这对“模型能力”这个概念本身的冲击，比分数更大。

Google open-sources Harness²: leave the weights alone and let agents rewrite their own “shell”

Google has open-sourced Harness² (github.com/google-research/harness2; paper arXiv 2610.14224). Rather than change model parameters, it lets an agent rewrite the layer around the model—the “harness”: system prompt, guardrails…

Why it mattersWhen the weights are out of reach—closed APIs, expensive compute, catastrophic forgetting—rewriting the shell is almost the only path left. The numbers are worth remembering: after a single optimization step, WorkBuddy-Bench +11.5, JobBench +4.5 and LAB +11.8. The model did not change; the score did. That has a bigger impact on the very idea of “model capability” than on any single benchmark.
