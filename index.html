Google 发布 Gemini 4 Carbon：主打网络安全与长程任务，先给防御者用

Google 10 月 28 日发布 Gemini 4 Carbon，距离上一代 Gemini 4 Argon 只隔两周。Google 称这是其最强的前沿模型，重点提升编程、自主智能体任务和网络安全防御能力。公司称，Carbon 在更新后的 Artificial Analysis 智能指数 v5.0 上以 59 分排名第一，在 WebDev Arena 上提升 89 分、升至第四。

网络安全是这次的主打场景：Google 称 Carbon 在 SEC-bench Pro 漏洞发现测试中得分 88.2%，ExploitGym 利用测试 54.8%，CWE-bench 修复测试 71%。在内部测试中，它把无依据陈述比 Argon 减少约一半，多步工作流中的任务越界减少 26%、服务不足减少 29%。Carbon 本周先向网络安全防御者开放，随后再向开发者、企业和消费者推出。

价格方面，Google 给出每百万输入 token 2 美元、输出 10 美元的优惠价，缓存输入再打 95% 折扣；优惠期后恢复为输入 4 美元、输出 20 美元。

看点“网络安全最强模型”天然是双刃剑：同一套漏洞发现能力可以用于防御，也可以用于攻击。Google 先向防御者开放、再逐步放开，是在用部署节奏换安全窗口；但 88.2% 的漏洞发现得分也意味着，攻击方拿到同等能力只是时间问题。

横店与字节否认“15 万群演数据喂模型”：数据归横店，不用于训练大模型

针对“横店与字节合作，把 15 万名群演的肖像和声音喂给字节大模型”的传言，字节豆包公关负责人刘星 10 月 28 日回应称“严重失实”。他表示，引发讨论的是横店影视 AI 服务平台项目，目标是建设影视行业的公共数字基础设施，把影视基地实景等资产做成高质量数据集，帮助剧组更高效地选景、筹备和拍摄，也降低中小影视企业使用 AI 的门槛。

刘星澄清了三点：火山引擎在该项目中仅是技术方，所有数据资产归横店或授权方所有；涉及任何人的肖像和声音使用，都需要本人授权，不存在未经授权采集；本项目采集的数据不会用于训练字节大模型。公开资料显示，该项目目前处于中标阶段，将围绕影视行业数据集建设和配套技术平台展开。

看点这是一次典型的“数据授权”舆情：公众真正担心的不是 AI 能不能做影视，而是自己的脸和声音会不会在不知情的情况下变成训练数据。把“数据归谁、授权在哪、是否用于训练”三件事说清楚，比一句“传言失实”更能建立信任。

Anthropic 收购“一人生物公司” Mithril，用 Claude 推进罕见病药物

Anthropic 已收购生物科技初创公司 Mithril Medicine，正式进入罕见病药物研发。Mithril 只有创始人 Cody Tranbarger 一名全职员工，他借助 Claude 管理 11 家外部研究机构的合同、实验排期、物流和数据分析，在不到 18 个月里把两个罕见肌肉疾病项目推进到动物实验阶段。

研究团队开发了一种同时结合两类蛋白的双特异性抗体，试图恢复肌肉纤维与周围组织的连接。给 LAMA2 相关疾病小鼠注射原型抗体 6 周后，部分实验组的握力和肌肉力量约为对照组的 2 倍，存活率也有改善，但未恢复到正常小鼠水平。Claude 还在后续研究中分析了 113 个候选抗体的 341 页制造数据，不到 8 分钟就完成分类。

错误同样存在：Claude 写的邮件里出现过抗体数量记录错误，至少 3 封在人工审核后仍发给了外部机构。Anthropic 称这是“一人药物开发流水线”的首个案例，若候选物验证顺利，最早可能在 2028 年下半年启动人体临床试验；目前只是早期动物结果，人体安全性和疗效均未验证。

看点AI 在这里不是“替科学家想出新药”，而是把合同、排期、物流、数据整理这些吃掉大量时间的环节压缩给一个人。这确实能提高早期研发效率，但 3 封带错邮件说明：人工审核不是保险箱，关键数据必须有自动校验和责任人。

Google launches Gemini 4 Carbon, built for cybersecurity and long-horizon work

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon on October 28, just two weeks after Gemini 4 Argon. Google calls it its strongest frontier model, with gains in coding, autonomous agent tasks and cybersecurity defense. It says Carbon ranks first on the updated Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 with a score of 59 and jumped 89 points to fourth place on the WebDev Arena leaderboard.

Cybersecurity is the headline use case: Google says Carbon scores 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding vulnerabilities, 54.8% on ExploitGym for exploitation and 71% on CWE-bench for patching. In internal tests, it cut unsupported claims by about half versus Argon, reduced task overreach by 26% and under-service by 29% in multi-step workflows. Carbon opens first to cybersecurity defenders this week, then to developers, businesses and consumers.

Google is offering introductory pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input discounted by 95%; standard pricing later rises to $4 per million input and $20 per million output.

Why it mattersA “best cybersecurity model” is inherently dual-use: the same vulnerability-finding ability can defend or attack. Opening first to defenders buys a safety window, but an 88.2% vulnerability-detection score also means attackers getting equivalent capability is only a matter of time.

Hengdian and ByteDance deny claim that 150,000 extras’ data trained a model

Responding to a viral claim that Hengdian and ByteDance were feeding the likenesses and voices of 150,000 extras into a ByteDance model, Doubao PR head Liu Xing said on October 28 that the claim was “seriously false.” He said the project in question is the Hengdian Film and TV AI Service Platform, which aims to build public digital infrastructure for the industry: turning real filming locations and other assets into high-quality datasets to help crews scout, prepare and shoot more efficiently, and to lower the barrier to AI for smaller studios.

Liu clarified three points: Volcano Engine is only a technology provider, and all data assets belong to Hengdian or authorized parties; any use of a person’s likeness or voice requires that person’s authorization, and there is no unauthorized collection; and the data collected by the project will not be used to train ByteDance’s large models. Public records show the project is at the bidding stage and will cover film-industry datasets and supporting technology platforms.

Why it mattersThis is a textbook data-consent controversy: the public worry is not whether AI can help film production, but whether their faces and voices become training data without their knowledge. Clearly answering “who owns the data, where is consent, and is it used for training?” builds more trust than a bare denial.

10/28 14:14 DigitalToday ↗

Anthropic buys one-person biotech Mithril and pushes into rare-disease drugs with Claude

Anthropic has acquired Mithril Medicine, a biotech startup with only one full-time employee, founder Cody Tranbarger, and is moving into rare-disease drug development. Tranbarger used Claude to manage contracts with 11 external research institutions, experiment schedules, logistics and data analysis, advancing two rare muscular disease programs to animal testing in under 18 months.

The team developed a bispecific antibody designed to restore the connection between muscle fibers and surrounding tissue. After six weeks of treatment in mice with a LAMA2-related disease, some groups had about twice the grip strength and muscle strength of controls; survival improved but did not return to normal. Claude also analyzed 341 pages of manufacturing data on 113 candidate antibodies and classified them in under eight minutes.

Errors happened too: Claude-written emails misrecorded antibody quantities, and at least three were sent to outside institutions even after human review. Anthropic calls this the first “one-person drug development pipeline”; if candidate validation goes smoothly, human trials could begin as early as the second half of 2028. The results are early animal data, and human safety and efficacy are unproven.

Why it mattersAI is not inventing the drug here so much as absorbing the contracts, scheduling, logistics and data wrangling that consume a scientist’s time. That can speed early R&D, but three erroneous emails show human review is not a safe deposit box; critical data needs automated checks and an accountable owner.
