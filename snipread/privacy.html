我们处理哪些数据

摘读是一款在本机运行的阅读器。你导入的 PDF、批注、笔记、文件夹和阅读位置，都保存在你 Mac 上的摘读数据文件夹里（在「设置 → 数据」中可以看到具体位置）。这些数据不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

框选识别文字时，摘读调用 macOS 内置的文字识别功能，在你的 Mac 上完成识别，图片和识别结果不会离开设备。

What data we handle

SnipRead is a reader that runs on your Mac. The PDFs you import, your annotations, notes, folders and reading positions are saved in SnipRead’s data folder on your Mac (Settings → Data shows where). This data is never sent to us or to any third party.

When you box text to recognize it, SnipRead uses the text recognition built into macOS. Recognition happens on your Mac; neither the image nor the result leaves the device.

反馈

只有当你在摘读里打开「反馈」并点「提交」时，摘读才会联网，发送以下内容：

  • 你填写的反馈内容、反馈类型，以及勾选的想要的功能；
  • 你选择填写的邮箱（可不填）；
  • 摘读的版本号和操作系统名称（如「macOS」），方便排查问题。

这些内容通过第三方邮件服务 EmailJS 发送到开发者的邮箱，仅用于阅读和回复你的反馈，不会用于广告、追踪或出售给任何人。反馈邮件保存在开发者的邮箱里；如需删除，请写信告知。

反馈表单里勾选的「想要的功能」会随反馈一起发送，同样只用于决定接下来开发什么。

Feedback

SnipRead goes online only when you open Feedback and click Submit. It then sends:

  • the message, feedback type and any features you ticked;
  • your email address, if you chose to enter one;
  • the SnipRead version and operating system name (such as “macOS”), to help diagnose problems.

This is delivered to the developer’s mailbox through the third-party email service EmailJS, and is used only to read and reply to your feedback. It is never used for advertising or tracking, or sold to anyone. Feedback emails are kept in the developer’s mailbox; write to us to have yours deleted.

Features you tick under “Which features do you want?” are sent with your feedback and are likewise used only to decide what to build next.

你的控制权

  • 备份：复制「设置 → 数据」里显示的文件夹即可。
  • 删除：删除这本书、这个文件夹，或卸载摘读并删除数据文件夹，数据即被彻底清除。
  • 反馈：如需删除你发过的反馈邮件，请写信到 snipread@163.com。

Your control

  • Back up: copy the folder shown in Settings → Data.
  • Delete: delete a book or folder in the app, or remove SnipRead and its data folder to erase everything.
  • Feedback: to have feedback you sent deleted, write to snipread@163.com.

儿童

摘读不面向特定年龄群体收集任何个人信息。

变更

如果摘读处理数据的方式发生变化（例如加入任何可选的匿名统计），我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期，并在加入前通过 App 告知你，允许你随时关闭。

联系

对本政策有任何疑问，请写信到 snipread@163.com。

Children

SnipRead does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If the way SnipRead handles data changes (for example, if any optional anonymous statistics are added), we will update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app before the change, with a way to turn it off at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: snipread@163.com.
