开始使用Getting started

  1. 点「导入 PDF」，选一本扫描版 PDF。书会出现在书架上，可以拖进文件夹整理。
  2. 点书名打开阅读。摘读会记住每本书读到哪一页、用什么缩放比例。
  3. 在工具栏点「OCR 选区」，在页面上拖一个框，框里的文字会被识别出来，点「复制」即可粘贴到别处。
  4. 用工具栏里的高亮、矩形、画笔、文字给页面做批注；点左边的小箭头打开这本书的笔记。
  5. 需要分享批注时，点「导出」生成一份带批注的 PDF。
  1. Click “Import PDF” and pick a scanned PDF. It appears on your bookshelf; drag it into a folder to organize.
  2. Click a book to read it. SnipRead remembers the page and zoom where you stopped.
  3. Click “OCR region” in the toolbar and drag a box on the page. The text inside is recognized; click “Copy” to paste it anywhere.
  4. Use highlight, rectangle, pen and text tools to annotate. Click the small arrow on the left to open this book’s notes.
  5. To share your markup, click “Export” to save a PDF with your annotations.

缩放：触控板双指撑开放大、捏合缩小，或按住 ⌃ 滚动；底部也有放大、缩小和「适应宽度」按钮。

Zoom: pinch on the trackpad, or hold ⌃ and scroll; the bottom bar also has zoom buttons and Fit width.

常见问题FAQ

文字识别需要联网吗？会上传我的书吗？
不需要，也不会。识别使用 macOS 系统自带的文字识别，在你的 Mac 上完成，书页图片不会离开电脑。
能识别哪些文字？
简体中文、繁体中文和英文。印刷清晰的扫描页效果最好；模糊、倾斜严重或手写的内容可能有错字，复制前请核对。
为什么有的字识别得不对？
框选范围小一点（一句或一段）通常更准。竖排文字（如竖排古籍）目前不支持。
我的书和笔记存在哪里？怎么备份？
都存在你自己的电脑上。打开「设置 → 数据」可以看到所在文件夹，复制这个文件夹就是备份；也可以在那里换一个存放位置，书和笔记会一起搬过去。
文字版 PDF 能用吗？
能打开、批注和框选识别。摘读专为扫描版设计，文字版 PDF 的文字本来就能直接选中，用系统自带的「预览」也很方便。
要收费吗？
现有功能全部免费，以后也免费。将来可能推出付费的进阶功能，已经免费的功能不会收回。
Does text recognition need the internet? Are my books uploaded?
No, and no. Recognition uses the text recognition built into macOS and runs on your Mac; page images never leave your computer.
Which languages can it read?
Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English. Clean printed scans work best; blurry, heavily skewed or handwritten text may contain mistakes, so check before you copy.
Why are some characters wrong?
Boxing a smaller area (a sentence or a paragraph) is usually more accurate. Vertical text, as in vertically set classical books, isn’t supported yet.
Where are my books and notes stored? How do I back them up?
On your own Mac. Settings → Data shows the folder; copy it to back up. You can also move the library there, and everything moves with it.
Does it work with text PDFs?
Yes: you can open, annotate and box-recognize them. SnipRead is designed for scanned books; text PDFs are already selectable, and macOS Preview handles them well.
Is it free?
Every current feature is free and stays free. Paid advanced features may come later; nothing that is free now will be taken away.
