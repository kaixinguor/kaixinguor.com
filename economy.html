英国央行加息 25 个基点至 4%：8 比 1 通过，曼恩主张加 50bp

英国央行货币政策委员会 11 月 5 日宣布，将银行利率上调 25 个基点至 4%，8 票赞成、1 票反对；反对者 Catherine Mann 主张加 50 个基点至 4.25%。会议截至 11 月 4 日。

二轮效应尚未出现：食品通胀低于预期，部分企业靠对冲与固定价格合同吸收成本、压缩利润率。但两年期房贷基准利率自 9 月初已上行近 50 个基点；用于货币政策的国债存量 11 月 4 日为 3620 亿英镑。

看点这是一次保险式加息：能源把通胀顶到 5%，但二轮效应尚未出现，先动的是利率而不是缩表。成本落在 2027 年续约的房贷家庭与 2027 年工资谈判上；久期比幅度更重要——如果能源回落，这 25bp 可能被反向抹掉；如果不回落，4% 不是终点。

Why it mattersWhat fell was not this quarter's earnings but the discount rate and the regulatory premium. Bank of England raises Bank Rate 25bp to 4% on an 8-1 vote as Mann pushes for 50bp

The Bank of England's Monetary Policy Committee raised Bank Rate by 25 basis points to 4% on November 5, with eight votes in favour and one against. Catherine Mann preferred a 50bp increase to 4.25%. The meeting ended on November 4.

Energy is the driver. UK CPI inflation was 3.5% in September, with direct energy effects contributing about 1 percentage point. The Bank expects inflation around 4.5% in Q1 2027 and around 5% in Q2 before it fades. Over the 15 working days to October 27, front-month Brent averaged $108 a barrel and UK front-month gas 216 pence per therm, 38% and 81% above the July report.

Second-round effects have not shown up yet: food inflation is lower than expected, and some firms are absorbing costs through hedges, fixed-price contracts and thinner margins. But two-year mortgage rates have risen nearly 50bp since early September, and the stock of gilts held for monetary policy was £362 billion on November 4.

Governor Andrew Bailey said that without news easing energy risks there is a case for a further increase, while Alan Taylor stressed this is 'not the start of a hiking cycle' but a 'one-for-now' insurance move. The next meeting is on December 17.

Why it mattersThis is an insurance hike: energy pushes inflation toward 5%, second-round effects are still missing, and the Bank moved the price rather than the balance sheet. The cost lands on households refinancing mortgages in 2027 and on the 2027 wage round. Duration matters more than size: if energy retreats, the 25bp may be reversed; if not, 4% is not the end.

Six ministries issue 14 measures on elderly-care products, naming embodied AI and brain-computer interfaces

Six ministries, including Civil Affairs, the NDRC, MIIT, Commerce, the market regulator and the national ageing office, issued 14 measures on November 4 to develop consumption scenarios for products used by older people. The document was published on November 6.

It explicitly calls for accelerating embodied AI, large language models, brain-computer interfaces and exoskeletons in elderly care, including smart devices for disability care, rehabilitation and companionship, and smart service robots.

On the consumer side it promotes rent-to-own and combined rental-sale models to lower the cost of use. On the supply side it encourages using state-owned buildings and community facilities as existing sites, and supports public-build/private-operate and 'site partnership plus revenue sharing' arrangements.

The document also promises a standards framework for the elderly-products industry, a certification scheme for senior products, and clearer market entry, exit and complaint handling.

Why it mattersThe policy offers scenarios, not subsidies: it lowers entry costs with existing assets and leaves affordability to rental and revenue-sharing. Whether it lasts depends on whether local governments give up income from those assets, and on how long-term care insurance and medical insurance pay for smart devices. For companies, work out the payback period on the site before talking about the robot.
