央行隔夜逆回购连续净回笼：11 月 10 日投放 1,900 亿元，11 月 MLF 与买断式合计到期 1.8 万亿元

中国人民银行 11 月 10 日 09:20 公告，以固定利率、数量招标方式开展 1,900 亿元隔夜逆回购操作，操作利率 1.25%，投标量 1,900 亿元、中标量 1,900 亿元，全额满足了一级交易商需求。

前一日是净回笼。据中国证券报报道，11 月 9 日央行开展 1,820 亿元隔夜逆回购，当日有 2,260 亿元隔夜逆回购到期，净回笼 440 亿元；月初以来短期逆回购连续净回笼，节奏与月初规律相符。

真正要盯的是 11 月的到期与缴款表：中期借贷便利和买断式逆回购合计到期 1.8 万亿元，需观察续作力度；11 月政府债净融资预计超过 1.5 万亿元，比 10 月明显增加；同业存单到期与季节性信贷投放还会推高银行负债需求。税期是 11 月 16—18 日。

工具的分工也在变。政策利率「换锚」落地后，7 天期逆回购已从每个工作日操作的主要工具退为阶段性补位，承担跨税期、跨月的流动性职能；日常投放交给隔夜逆回购，长期投放则靠国债买卖——10 月国债买卖净投放 1,500 亿元，是再度启用后的新高。

看点利率没动，久期在动。隔夜工具占日常投放的比重越高，银行负债端的平均期限越短，流动性管理的难度从「价格」转到「滚动」：只要央行某天少做一点，资金面就会立刻感到紧。如果 1.8 万亿元到期不续作、1.5 万亿元政府债净融资又全部由银行体系吸收，两项合计相当于抽走约 3.3 万亿元准备金（按 300 万亿元存款粗略折算，接近一次 1.1 个百分点的降准所释放的规模），续作力度决定了 10 年期国债收益率在 11 月是继续下行还是被打断。
另一个视角对做负债的银行司库，真正的成本不是 1.25% 的隔夜利率，而是每天都要续作的确定性；对做久期的债券基金，杠杆成本跟着隔夜走，一旦税期资金利率跳升，carry 会被迅速吃掉。

储蓄国债今天开卖：3 年期 1.63%、5 年期 1.7%，两期合计 553.43 亿元

财政部与中国人民银行 11 月 9 日发布财债〔2026〕44 号文：2026 年第九期储蓄国债（电子式）期限 3 年，票面年利率 1.63%，最大发行额 249.04 亿元；第十期期限 5 年，票面年利率 1.7%，最大发行额 304.39 亿元。两期合计 553.43 亿元，发行期 11 月 10 日至 11 月 19 日，11 月 10 日起息、按年付息。

看点把 1.63% 和 1.7% 放到 10 月 CPI 同比 0.7% 上，实际收益率大约是 0.93 和 1.0 个百分点——这是财政用居民储蓄融资的真实价格。钱从居民存款搬到财政部账户，记在政府债务表上，成本是刚性的固定利率：利率不变、额度略减，说明这一批更像「维持发行节奏」而不是「加大吸储」。对银行，储蓄国债是存款的替代品，1.7% 的五年期会直接分流长期定存；对居民，5 年期 1.7% 锁住的是名义收益，通胀一旦回到 2% 以上，实际收益就转负。

11/10 09:20 People's Bank of China ↗

Overnight reverse repos keep draining: RMB 190bn lent on November 10, with RMB 1.8tn of MLF and outright repos maturing in November

The People's Bank of China said at 09:20 on November 10 that it conducted RMB 190bn of overnight reverse repos through fixed-rate, quantity-tender operations at an unchanged 1.25%. Bids and allotments were both RMB 190bn, fully meeting the demand of primary dealers.

The previous day was a net drain. China Securities Journal reported that the central bank lent RMB 182bn overnight on November 9 against RMB 226bn maturing, a net withdrawal of RMB 44bn; short-tenor repos have now drained cash for several sessions, in line with the usual start-of-month pattern.

The calendar matters more than the daily print. Medium-term lending facility loans and outright reverse repos mature to the tune of RMB 1.8tn in November, so the rollover ratio is the number to watch. Net government bond financing is expected to exceed RMB 1.5tn, a clear step up from October, while maturing negotiable certificates of deposit and seasonal credit demand add to bank funding needs. The tax payment window runs from November 16 to 18.

The toolkit is being re-sorted. Since the policy rate re-anchoring, seven-day reverse repos have stepped back from being the daily workhorse to a fill-in tool for tax periods and month-end, while overnight operations handle routine liquidity and government bond trading handles the long end: net purchases of government bonds reached RMB 150bn in October, the highest since the tool was reactivated.

Why it mattersThe rate has not moved; the tenor has. The more routine liquidity is supplied overnight, the shorter banks' liability duration becomes, and the constraint shifts from price to rollover: one quiet day from the central bank is felt immediately. If none of the RMB 1.8tn is rolled over and banks absorb all of the RMB 1.5tn of net government bond issuance, the two together withdraw roughly RMB 3.3tn of reserves — on a RMB 300tn deposit base, close to what a 1.1 percentage point cut in the reserve requirement ratio would release. The rollover decision will therefore decide whether the 10-year yield keeps falling through November or stalls.
Another viewFor a bank treasurer the cost is not the 1.25% overnight rate but the certainty of having to roll it every morning; for a duration-taking bond fund, the leverage cost tracks that overnight rate, and a tax-period spike eats the carry quickly.

It is not isolated. On the same disclosure page of the newspaper, Guotai's S&P 500 ETF (QDII) published its own premium warning. The notices also remind investors that secondary prices reflect supply and demand, systemic risk and liquidity as well as NAV.

The mechanism is simple. Primary creation in a cross-border ETF is limited by foreign exchange quota, so when quota is tight and subscriptions are restricted, buyers can only bid up the secondary market, and the price decouples from NAV. The notice does not disclose the fund's current subscription status, saying only that operations are normal and there is nothing else material to disclose.

Why it mattersA 21% premium is not a 21% gain in the portfolio; it is 21% paid for access. That money does not enter fund assets or the index constituents. It sits in the buyer's cost basis, and the buyer can only recover it by finding someone willing to pay a larger premium — a partly zero-sum position. A one-hour halt interrupts trading without changing quota-bound supply and demand, so the premium usually narrows after the reopen, and that narrowing is the loss of those who bought it. For the manager, the premium brings scale and attention but weakens the stability of the holder base; for regulators, how cross-border quota is allocated determines whether this premium keeps recurring.
