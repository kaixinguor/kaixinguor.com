央行把政策利率锚在隔夜：11 月 2 日起每日招标 1.25% 逆回购

10 月 30 日 17 时，央行公开市场业务操作室发布〔2026〕第 13 号公告：为进一步优化短端利率调控机制，从 2026 年 11 月 2 日起调整公开市场操作。

一是每个工作日开展隔夜逆回购操作招标，采用固定利率、数量招标，目前操作利率为 1.25%。二是 7 天期逆回购调整为固定数量、利率招标、多重价位中标，主要在税期、月末等时点开展。三是原在 7 天期逆回购利率基础上加减点的其他工具，包括临时正/逆回购、境外央行类机构回购工具等，均改为在隔夜逆回购利率基础上加减点，加减点幅度不变。

换句话说，每天的资金价格由隔夜品种定，7 天品种退为应付税期和月末的流动性工具；政策利率到市场利率的传导链，缩短了一个期限。

看点把锚从 7 天挪到隔夜，改的不只是报价，而是「谁来决定每天的钱价」。隔夜每天到期、每天续作，央行可以逐日微调，市场则要逐日重新借钱——短端波动会更频繁，机构的流动性管理从「锁一周」变成「滚一天」。对定价的影响是具体的：同业存单、短债、浮息贷款的基准要重新找锚，利率走廊的上下限随加减点平移。真正的考验在第一个月末：如果隔夜利率在税期跳升，这套框架就要回答「是工具不够，还是传导没通」。

10/30 新华财经 ↗

信托新规落地：不许刚兑、不许预期收益率、不许资金池

10 月 30 日，国家金融监督管理总局发布《资产管理信托管理办法》。办法明确：信托公司管理信托产品，不得以任何方式向投资者承诺本金不受损失或者保证最低收益，不得设置预期收益率。

同时禁止四类做法：不得以信托产品名义开展为融资方募资服务的投资银行业务；不得开展或参与具有滚动发行、集合运作、分离定价特征的资金池业务；不得为其他机构或个人从事违法违规活动提供便利；不得进行转委托，不得将管理职责让渡给其他机构或个人。

办法还要求遵守公平交易原则，不得在不同信托之间、信托与信托公司或第三方之间进行非法利益输送；依法委托其他方处理信托事务的，信托公司仍须履行监督义务，不能因委托而免责。

看点刚兑、资金池、通道，是过去信托业的三条腿，这次被同一份文件同时拆掉。对融资方，意味着借道信托拿钱的成本上升、期限变短；对投资者，意味着「预期收益率」这个心理锚消失，产品净值化真正开始。风险不会消失，只会换位置：从信托产品背后挪到债券和贷款的持有人身上。同一天交易所批出 366 亿元房企项目债，一收一放，指向的是同一个方向——融资从表外搬到表内，从主体信用搬到项目现金流。

The PBoC re-anchors policy at overnight: 1.25% reverse repos, every working day from November 2

At 5 p.m. on October 30, the PBoC’s open-market operations room issued Announcement No. 13 of 2026: to further improve the short-end interest-rate framework, open-market operations will change from November 2, 2026.

First, an overnight reverse repo will be tendered every working day at a fixed rate and quantity, with the current rate at 1.25%. Second, the seven-day reverse repo becomes a fixed-quantity, rate-based auction with multi-price allotment, used mainly around tax periods and month-end. Third, tools that previously added or subtracted a spread to the seven-day rate — including temporary repo and reverse repo, and the repo facility for foreign central-bank-type institutions — will be priced off the overnight rate instead, with the spread unchanged.

In practice, the daily price of money will be set at the overnight tenor, while the seven-day operation becomes a tool for tax dates and month-end. The transmission chain from the policy rate to market rates loses one maturity.

Why it mattersMoving the anchor changes more than a quoted rate: it changes who sets the daily price of money. Overnight funding matures and rolls every day, so the central bank can adjust daily and the market must re-borrow daily. Short-end volatility becomes more frequent, and liquidity management shifts from locking in a week to rolling a day. The pricing effects are concrete: negotiable certificates of deposit, short bonds and floating-rate loans all need a new reference, and the rate corridor moves with the spread. The real test comes at the first month-end under the new regime — if overnight rates jump at tax time, the framework will have to answer whether the tool was insufficient or transmission was blocked.

New trust rules: no principal guarantees, no expected returns, no pooled funds

On October 30, the National Financial Regulatory Administration issued the Measures for the Administration of Asset-Management Trusts. Trust companies may not promise investors that principal will not be lost or guarantee a minimum return in any way, and may not set an expected rate of return.

The rules also ban four practices: using trust products to run investment-banking business that raises money for financiers; operating or joining pooled funds characterized by rolling issuance, collective operation and separated pricing; facilitating illegal or non-compliant activity by other institutions or individuals; and re-delegating management or ceding management responsibility to another institution or person.

Trust companies must also follow fair dealing and may not transfer benefits illegally between trusts, or between a trust and the company or a third party. Where other parties are lawfully engaged to handle trust affairs, the trust company must supervise them and cannot be relieved of liability by delegating.

Why it mattersPrincipal guarantees, pooled funds and channel business were the three legs of the trust industry, and one document removes all three at once. For borrowers, raising money through trusts gets more expensive and shorter-dated; for investors, the psychological anchor of an “expected return” disappears and products genuinely move to net asset value. Risk does not vanish, it changes address — from behind trust products to the holders of bonds and loans. The same day the exchange approved RMB 36.6 billion of developer project bonds: one door closes, another opens, and both point the same way — financing moves on-balance-sheet and from entity credit to project cash flow.
