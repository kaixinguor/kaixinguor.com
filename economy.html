货币与政策

11/02 06:48 经济日报 ↗

财政部提前下达 2027 年部分新增地方债限额：上限 60%，1 月即可发债

财政部近日明确，将抓紧做好 2027 年部分新增地方政府债务限额提前下达工作，确保明年 1 月各地尽早启动债券发行。每年第四季度在当年新增限额 60% 以内提前下达下一年度额度，是经全国人大常委会授权确立的制度安排。

按照法律规定，地方政府新增债务遵循 3 月全国人大审批全年限额、财政部分解下达、地方启动发行的流程，债券资金真正落地往往要到二季度以后。提前下达的作用，是让地方在四季度就完成项目储备、预算编制与前期审批，次年 1 月即可发债、一季度资金到位。

这项授权来自 2018 年 12 月与 2023 年 10 月全国人大常委会的两次决定，现行授权有效期至 2027 年 12 月 31 日。上一次实施是 2025 年 11 月提前下达部分 2026 年新增专项债；2026 年一季度全国共发行 11599 亿元，同比增长超两成。全年新增债务规模仍须全国人大审查批准，提前下达只解决「时间」，不改变「总量」。

看点钱从「等额度」变成「等开工」。过去一季度常见的错配是资金与项目对不上：额度没批、项目没备，等到二季度钱到了，施工窗口已经过半。提前下达把这段时滞压掉，代价是地方要在四季度就把项目清单做实——项目质量不够，提前批反而会变成「钱等项目」。对债市，1 月供给前置、二三季度供给被平滑，发行利率更容易压住；对地方财政，付息压力并没有减少，只是提前一个季度开始计息。真正要观察的是 2027 年一季度的实物工作量，而不是发行规模。

11/01 20:25 经济日报 ↗

沪深三季报：净利 5.1 万亿元、增 8.5%，AI 板块净利增 281%

据中国证监会透露，截至 10 月 31 日，沪深两市 5224 家上市公司披露了 2026 年三季报。前三季度合计营业收入 56.6 万亿元、净利润 5.1 万亿元，同比分别增长 5.8%、8.5%，规模及增速均创 2022 年以来新高；73% 的上市公司实现盈利，滚动净资产收益率（ROE）为 7.9%。

结构分化比总量更明显：科创板上市公司净利润增长 474.6%，创业板增长 22.7%。以人工智能、机器人、创新药为代表的新质生产力公司业绩高速增长，其中人工智能板块净利润同比增长 281.0%，增速较半年报加快 14.5 个百分点；机器人、智能穿戴设备领域净利润分别增长 94.8%、63.2%。

把两个增速放在一起看：营收增 5.8%，净利增 8.5%，利润增速高于营收增速，说明利润率在修复。但修复并不均匀——73% 的公司盈利，意味着仍有约四分之一在亏损或微利；科创板 474.6% 的增速建立在低基数上，AI 板块 281% 同样有算力需求集中释放的成分。

看点这份成绩单的关键不是 5.1 万亿元，而是「利润从哪里来」。营收只增 5.8%，净利却增 8.5%，差额来自成本端：上游价格回落、费用控制、以及高毛利的新质生产力业务占比上升。AI 板块 281% 的增速最耀眼，但要拆成两块看——一块是算力硬件量价齐升，另一块是应用端收入，前者随资本开支波动，后者才是可持续的。对配置，73% 的盈利面比总量更重要，它决定了行情是普涨还是结构；对政策，科创板 474.6% 说明制度红利与产业周期叠加，但低基数效应会在明年一季度消失，届时才是真正的压力测试。

11/02 新华财经（中国证券报） ↗

前三季度物流总额 280 万亿元、增 4.9%，物流费用占 GDP 降至 13.8%

中国物流与采购联合会 11 月 1 日发布数据：前三季度全国社会物流总额 280.0 万亿元，按可比价格计算同比增长 4.9%；一、二、三季度分别增长 6.2%、4.4%、4.7%。社会物流总费用 14.8 万亿元，同比增长 4.2%；与 GDP 的比率降至 13.8%，比上年同期回落 0.2 个百分点。

按每创造 100 元 GDP 所支出的物流费用测算，0.2 个百分点的累计降幅对应节约物流费用超过 2100 亿元。中国物流信息中心称，物流运行呈现「稳中有进、质效双升、动能向新、结构向优」的态势，降本红利持续向实体经济释放。

需求结构上，工业品物流总额同比增长 5.3%，对物流总额增长的贡献率达 78%；装备制造业和高技术制造业比重分别升至 37.6% 和 18.3%。集成电路制造、航天器及运载火箭制造、光电子器件制造相关物流需求分别增长 79.2%、36.2% 和 28.4%。消费端，单位与居民物品物流总额增长 2.9%，网上商品零售额增长 3.8%，即时零售交易额保持两位数增长。

资金端也有动作：近期盘活 2500 亿元地方政府专项债务结存限额，支持「六张网」等重点领域建设，年内重点领域投资将超过 7 万亿元。前三季度物流业总收入 11.1 万亿元，同比增长 4.3%；物流业景气指数均值 50.3%。

看点2100 亿元不是统计口径变出来的，是从运输、仓储、口岸各环节的费率与空驶率里挤出来的，落在货主（制造业）账上。物流费用率下降也不等于需求强：二季度 4.4% 是低点、三季度回到 4.7%，说明生产在恢复，但恢复的斜率很平。真正值得注意的是结构——集成电路物流增 79.2%、电子元件出口增 70%，AI 供应链是增量，而单位与居民物品只增 2.9%，消费端仍是弱项。对四季度，双十一与年末赶工会推高总额，但价格战会压物流业收入；对政策，2500 亿元结存限额是「存量额度再利用」，不新增限额，等于用旧债额度给新投资供血。

11/02 01:22 证券日报 ↗

10 月百城新房价格环比涨 0.18%，二手房同比跌 6.72%

中指研究院数据显示，10 月全国 100 个城市新建住宅平均价格 17316 元/平方米，环比上涨 0.18%、同比上涨 2.02%；二手住宅平均价格 12376 元/平方米，环比下跌 0.61%、同比下跌 6.72%。

成交端却是另一幅图景。安居客统计，重点 20 城 10 月二手房成交量同比上涨 20%，增速较 9 月明显抬升，二手房用户热度环比增长 34.3%。上海房产交易中心数据显示，10 月上海二手房网签量超 2.4 万套，环比增长 4%、同比增长 32%；新房网签量超 7000 套，环比下降 37%、同比增长 5%。

政策背景是 9 月末财政部等三部门发布的《关于实施居民购房贷款贴息政策的通知》。58 安居客研究院院长张波称，本轮行情受《关于完善商品住房销售制度的通知》与贴息政策累积释放影响，贴息对三四线城市覆盖率可达 44% 以上；同时现房销售制度拉长房企开发周期，短期压制新房新增供应，把更多需求挤向二手房，传导效应或在 2027 年集中显现。

看点贴息的钱由财政出、息差由银行收，等于财政替居民付一部分利息，把需求从「观望」推到「成交」。但价格跌的是二手、量涨的也是二手——政策改变了成交量，没有改变定价权：二手房同比仍跌 6.72%，说明卖方仍在让价换流动性。新房环比涨 0.18% 是结构性的，涨的是核心区优质盘，不是普涨。对地方财政，二手活跃替代不了土地出让；对房企，现房销售把回款周期拉长，2027 年的供应收缩才是下一个变量。要判断这轮行情能不能持续，看的是二手挂牌量而不是网签量。

11/01 18:47 中国证券报 ↗

10 月新势力交付分化：零跑 10.61 万辆、小鹏 5.02 万辆创新高

11 月 1 日，多家新势力车企发布 10 月交付数据，分化明显：零跑汽车交付 10.61 万辆，同比增长 51%、环比增长 0.45%，连续 4 个月月交付超 10 万辆，稳居榜首；小鹏集团交付 5.02 万辆，同比增长 20%、环比增长 22%，单月交付创历史新高，其中海外交付 13326 辆，同比增长 166%。

小米汽车 10 月交付超过 5 万辆，创今年以来新高。蔚来交付 3.42 万辆，同比下滑 15.23%、环比下滑约 8.45%（蔚来品牌 1.81 万辆、乐道 8383 辆、firefly 萤火虫 7738 辆）；理想汽车交付 2.9 万辆，同比、环比降幅均超 8%；极氪交付 3.25 万辆，同比增长 51.6%、环比下滑 12.7%；鸿蒙智行交付 3.35 万辆，环比下滑 10.54%。

出海与本地化是这一轮的分水岭。中汽协副秘书长陈士华称，今年新能源汽车出口占比已连续四个月超过 50%。零跑 B10 在西班牙萨拉戈萨工厂启动量产，零跑在西班牙布局两座工厂（萨拉戈萨负责整车、马连负责电池模组）；小鹏 10 月 26 日获批广州全域 L4 级 Robotaxi 示范运营资格。

资本动作也在同步：理想旗下芯片子公司上海马赫心科技完成首轮 16.25 亿元融资，由杭州经韬、蓝驰创投、华业天成领投，奇绩创坛参与；杭州经韬及关联基金获最高 5 亿元追加认购权，若悉数行使，投后估值 171 亿元。

看点交付表上的分化，本质是「规模—成本—出海」三条曲线的位置不同。零跑用 10 万辆级的规模摊薄成本，小鹏用海外 13326 辆、+166% 拿溢价，理想与蔚来则在新旧车型切换期承担双降；同样打价格战，前两家是「以量补价」，后两家是「量价齐跌」。更值得看的是出海方式：零跑把产能放到西班牙，绕开关税与交付周期；小鹏把 Robotaxi 资质拿在广州，卖的是运营许可而不是车。理想把芯片子公司单独融资，是把资本开支外部化——16.25 亿元进表，亏损和估值留在子公司。四季度要盯的不是交付总量，而是欧洲本地化产能的爬坡速度与单车毛利。

41 家券商三季报：前三季净利增 24%，三季度单季降 16.86%

截至 10 月 31 日，41 家上市券商三季报披露完毕。据 Wind 数据，前三季度合计营业收入 4924.42 亿元，同比增长 19.16%；合计归母净利润 2065.74 亿元，同比增长 24.02%，约六成实现营收净利双增。

头部集中度继续上升：8 家券商归母净利润突破百亿元。中信证券以 310.22 亿元居首，国泰海通 248.85 亿元位列第二；招商证券、广发证券、华泰证券分别约 172.82 亿元、160.27 亿元、150.41 亿元；中金公司、中国银河、中信建投分别约 132.45 亿元、110.25 亿元、102.57 亿元。

但单季数据是另一回事。第三季度 41 家券商合计营业收入 1326.72 亿元，同比下滑 19.88%；合计归母净利润 533.26 亿元，同比下滑 16.86%，仅 7 家实现营收净利双增。招商证券、中金公司、华安证券第三季度归母净利润分别增长 80.65%、125.63%、251.35%，前三季度增幅达到 94.81%、101.71%、169.56%。

增长的主要来源是科创投资，而不是经纪或投行。半导体公司长鑫科技 7 月 27 日登陆科创板，发行价 8.66 元/股，9 月 30 日收盘 54.79 元/股，期间涨幅超过 5 倍；中金公司、中信建投是保荐人与主承销商，招商证券与华安证券则通过穿透持股受益。投行业务前三季度净收入合计约 314.91 亿元，前五名合计约 190.54 亿元，占比超过六成。

看点前三季度 24% 的净利增长，含金量要打折：它主要来自一级市场的浮盈，而不是可持续的经纪、资管或投行费率。三季度单季 -16.86% 才是真实经营状态的切片——行情转向后，自营盘先受伤，41 家里只有 7 家双增。科创投资是一次性收益，长鑫科技这类项目退出后，明年不会自动再来一个。对估值，券商 PB 取决于 ROE 的可持续性，而现在的 ROE 靠项目退出撑着；对投资者，要看的是四季度自营的方向性敞口与投行项目储备，而不是前三季度的累计增速。头部集中度上升也是同一枚硬币：项目资源向保荐能力强的券商集中，中小券商只能靠自营博弹性。

OPEC+ 七国决定 12 月维持产量不变，2027 配额谈判留到四季度

石油输出国组织（欧佩克）11 月 1 日发表声明说，7 个「欧佩克+」主要产油国决定 12 月继续维持当前原油产量不变，与 9 月水平相同。沙特阿拉伯、俄罗斯、伊拉克、科威特、哈萨克斯坦、阿尔及利亚和阿曼的代表当天举行线上会议，研判全球石油市场现状与前景。

今年 4 月至 9 月，主要产油国逐月增产；10 月起暂停增产。声明并未提及维持产量不变的原因，但有媒体报道，「欧佩克+」产油国仍在努力应对中东冲突造成的出口中断，因此在明年之前不会进行更多政策调整。

更远的安排是产能评估：各产油国需要在第四季度完成生产能力评估，进而确定 2027 年的产量配额。这意味着 12 月的决定只是把今年的产量曲线收尾，明年的配额才是下一轮博弈的起点。

看点「维持不变」的作用不是增加供应，而是把「增产预期」从市场里拿走。4 月到 9 月的逐月增产已经让闲置产能下降，10 月起暂停、12 月延续，等于把价格的下限交给闲置产能、上限交给地缘风险。对国内，成品油调价参考的原油变化率短期会更稳，炼厂的裂解价差少了一个扰动项；对 2027 年，第四季度的产能评估才是关键——它决定各国明年的配额基数，也决定「限产保价」还能不能维持。风险在实物端：如果中东出口中断扩大，纸面配额与现货供应会脱节，油价的地缘溢价就不是配额能压住的。

Beijing brings the 2027 local-bond quota forward: 60% cap, issuance from January

The Ministry of Finance will move early to allocate part of the 2027 local-government debt quota, so provinces can start bond sales in January. The early allocation is capped at 60% of the current year’s new quota and rests on authorisation from the National People’s Congress Standing Committee.

Under the normal process, the NPC approves the annual quota in March, the ministry allocates it, and localities issue; money often reaches projects only after the second quarter. Early allocation lets localities prepare projects, budgets and approvals in the fourth quarter, so bonds can be sold in January and funds can reach projects in the first quarter.

The authorisation dates from December 2018 and October 2023 and runs through December 31, 2027. The last round came in November 2025 for part of the 2026 special-bond quota; in the first quarter of 2026, localities issued ¥1.1599 trillion, up more than 20% year on year. The full-year ceiling still requires NPC approval; early allocation changes timing, not the total.

Why it mattersThe money moves from waiting for a quota to waiting for a shovel. The first-quarter mismatch has been that quotas and projects were not ready when the construction window opened; early allocation compresses that lag, at the price of requiring localities to have a credible project list in the fourth quarter — if project quality is weak, the early quota becomes cash waiting for projects. For bonds, supply front-loads into January and smooths across the year, making it easier to keep issuance yields down; for local finances, interest starts accruing a quarter earlier. Watch first-quarter 2027 physical construction, not the issuance total.

Shanghai and Shenzhen results: ¥5.1 trillion of profit, up 8.5%, with AI up 281%

By October 31, 5,224 Shanghai and Shenzhen listed companies had reported 2026 third-quarter results, according to the CSRC. Revenue for the first nine months totalled ¥56.6 trillion, up 5.8% year on year; net profit was ¥5.1 trillion, up 8.5%, with both scale and growth the highest since 2022. 73% of companies were profitable, and trailing ROE was 7.9%.

The split matters more than the total: net profit at STAR Market companies rose 474.6%, and at ChiNext companies 22.7%. AI, robotics and innovative drugs led. AI net profit rose 281.0%, 14.5 percentage points faster than in the half-year report; robotics rose 94.8% and smart wearables 63.2%.

Revenue grew 5.8% while profit grew 8.5%, so margins expanded. But the recovery is uneven: with 73% profitable, roughly a quarter are still losing money or barely breaking even; the STAR Market’s 474.6% rests on a low base, and AI’s 281% includes a concentrated release of compute demand.

Why it mattersThe story is not the ¥5.1 trillion but where the profit came from. Revenue rose only 5.8%; the extra profit came from costs — softer input prices, expense control, and a larger share of high-margin new-economy business. AI’s 281% needs splitting: hardware volume and price versus application revenue. The former swings with capex; the latter is durable. For allocation, the 73% profitability rate matters more than the total because it decides whether the market broadens or stays structural. For policy, the STAR Market number shows institutional dividends meeting an industry cycle, but the low-base effect disappears in the first quarter of 2027 — that is the real stress test.

Logistics value reaches ¥280 trillion, up 4.9%, as the cost-to-GDP ratio falls to 13.8%

China Federation of Logistics and Purchasing data released on November 1 show social logistics value of ¥280.0 trillion in the first nine months, up 4.9% in comparable prices, with quarterly growth of 6.2%, 4.4% and 4.7%. Total logistics costs were ¥14.8 trillion, up 4.2%; the cost-to-GDP ratio fell to 13.8%, down 0.2 percentage points year on year.

At ¥100 of GDP, that 0.2-point drop implies more than ¥210 billion of logistics costs saved. The federation says the sector is “stable with progress, better quality and efficiency, new drivers and a better structure”, with the savings passed to the real economy.

Industrial logistics rose 5.3% and contributed 78% of total growth; equipment manufacturing and high-tech manufacturing rose to 37.6% and 18.3% of the total. Logistics demand linked to integrated-circuit manufacturing, spacecraft and launch vehicles, and optoelectronic devices grew 79.2%, 36.2% and 28.4%. Consumer logistics rose 2.9%, online goods retail rose 3.8%, and instant retail grew at a double-digit rate.

On funding, ¥250 billion of unused local-government special-bond quota has been mobilised for the “six networks” and other priorities, with investment in key areas set to exceed ¥7 trillion this year. Logistics revenue reached ¥11.1 trillion, up 4.3%, and the logistics prosperity index averaged 50.3.

Why it mattersThe ¥210 billion is not a statistical artefact; it comes out of freight rates, warehousing and port charges and empty running, and lands on manufacturers’ books. A lower cost ratio does not mean strong demand: growth bottomed at 4.4% in the second quarter and recovered to 4.7% in the third — a recovery, but a flat one. The structure is the interesting part: integrated-circuit logistics up 79.2% and electronic-component exports up 70% show AI supply chains as the increment, while consumer logistics grew only 2.9%. For the fourth quarter, Double 11 and year-end production will lift volumes, but price competition will squeeze logistics revenue. The ¥250 billion is recycled quota, not new borrowing — old authority funding new investment.

New-home prices edge up 0.18% in October while second-hand prices fall 6.72%

China Index Academy data show the average new-home price in 100 cities at ¥17,316 per square metre in October, up 0.18% month on month and 2.02% year on year. Second-hand prices averaged ¥12,376, down 0.61% on the month and 6.72% on the year.

Transactions tell a different story. Anjuke says second-hand home transactions in 20 key cities rose 20% year on year in October, with user interest up 34.3% from September. In Shanghai, more than 24,000 second-hand units were registered, up 4% on the month and 32% on the year; new-home registrations exceeded 7,000, down 37% on the month but up 5% on the year.

Policy is doing the work. At the end of September, the Ministry of Finance and two other departments issued a notice on interest subsidies for residential mortgages. Zhang Bo of Anjuke says the market reflects the combined effect of the notice on housing sales rules and the subsidy, which covers more than 44% of tier-3 and tier-4 cities; at the same time, the shift to finished-home sales lengthens developers’ cycles, suppresses new supply and pushes demand into the second-hand market, with the effect likely concentrated in 2027.

Why it mattersThe subsidy has the government paying part of the interest and banks keeping the spread — fiscal money buying transactions. But prices are falling and volumes rising in the same second-hand market: policy changed the quantity, not the pricing power, since second-hand prices are still down 6.72%. The 0.18% new-home gain is structural, driven by good projects in core districts, not a broad rise. For local finances, a lively second-hand market does not replace land sales; for developers, finished-home sales stretch cash collection, and the 2027 supply contraction is the next variable. To judge whether this lasts, watch listings, not registrations.

October deliveries split: Leapmotor tops 106,000 and Xpeng hits a record

On November 1, China’s new-energy vehicle startups reported October deliveries, and the split was stark. Leapmotor delivered 106,100 vehicles, up 51% year on year and 0.45% month on month, its fourth straight month above 100,000 and enough to stay on top. Xpeng delivered 50,200, up 20% year on year and 22% on the month, a monthly record, including 13,326 overseas units, up 166%.

Xiaomi delivered more than 50,000, its best month this year. Nio delivered 34,200, down 15.23% year on year and about 8.45% on the month (Nio brand 18,100, Onvo 8,383, firefly 7,738); Li Auto delivered 29,000, down more than 8% on both counts; Zeekr delivered 32,500, up 51.6% year on year but down 12.7% on the month; Harmony Intelligent Mobility delivered 33,500, down 10.54% from September.

Overseas production and localisation are the dividing line. Chen Shihua, deputy secretary-general of the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers, says new-energy vehicle exports have accounted for more than 50% of the total for four consecutive months. Leapmotor’s B10 entered production at its Zaragoza plant in Spain; the company runs two Spanish sites, one for vehicles and one for battery modules. Xpeng won a licence on October 26 to run L4 robotaxi demonstrations across Guangzhou.

Capital moves too: Li Auto’s chip subsidiary Shanghai Mahexin Technology raised RMB 1.625 billion in a first round led by Hangzhou Jingtao, BlueRun Ventures and Huaye Tiancheng, with MiraclePlus participating; Hangzhou Jingtao and affiliates hold warrants for up to RMB 500 million more, which would value the company at RMB 17.1 billion post-money.

Why it mattersThe delivery table shows three different positions on the scale-cost-export curve. Leapmotor spreads costs over 100,000 units a month; Xpeng earns a premium abroad with 13,326 units, up 166%; Nio and Li Auto are absorbing double declines during model transitions. All four are fighting a price war, but the first two are trading price for volume while the latter two lose both. The more interesting difference is how they go abroad: Leapmotor is putting capacity in Spain to avoid tariffs and shorten delivery, while Xpeng is selling an operating licence in Guangzhou rather than a car. Li Auto’s chip spin-out externalises capex — RMB 1.625 billion comes in while losses and valuation stay at the subsidiary. For the fourth quarter, watch the ramp of European local capacity and per-vehicle gross margin, not total deliveries.

41 brokers: nine-month profit up 24%, third quarter down 16.86%

All 41 listed brokers reported by October 31. Wind data show first-nine-month revenue of ¥492.442 billion, up 19.16%, and net profit attributable to shareholders of ¥206.574 billion, up 24.02%; about 60% grew both lines.

Concentration rose: eight brokers earned more than ¥10 billion. CITIC led at ¥31.022 billion, Guotai Haitong followed at ¥24.885 billion; CMS, GF and Huatai earned about ¥17.282 billion, ¥16.027 billion and ¥15.041 billion; CICC, China Galaxy and CSC earned about ¥13.245 billion, ¥11.025 billion and ¥10.257 billion.

The single quarter was different. Third-quarter revenue across the 41 fell 19.88% year on year to ¥132.672 billion, and net profit fell 16.86% to ¥53.326 billion; only seven grew both lines. CMS, CICC and Huaan grew third-quarter net profit by 80.65%, 125.63% and 251.35%, taking nine-month growth to 94.81%, 101.71% and 169.56%.

The driver was tech investment, not brokerage or underwriting. Memory maker CXMT listed on the STAR Market on July 27 at ¥8.66 a share and closed at ¥54.79 on September 30, more than a fivefold gain; CICC and CSC were sponsors and lead underwriters, while CMS and Huaan benefited through holdings. Investment-banking fees were about ¥31.491 billion, with the top five taking ¥19.054 billion, over 60%.

Why it mattersThe 24% nine-month profit growth deserves a discount: it came mainly from mark-to-market gains on primary-market holdings, not from durable brokerage, asset-management or underwriting fees. The third-quarter decline of 16.86% is the true operating snapshot — when the market turned, proprietary books took the hit first, and only seven of 41 grew both lines. Tech investment is one-off; after CXMT-type exits, next year will not automatically bring another. For valuation, broker PB depends on sustainable ROE, and today’s ROE leans on exits. Watch fourth-quarter proprietary exposure and the investment-banking pipeline, not the nine-month cumulative. Rising concentration is the same coin: deal flow goes to sponsors with execution strength, while smaller brokers bet on proprietary volatility.

Seven OPEC+ producers hold December output steady, leaving 2027 quotas for the fourth quarter

OPEC said on November 1 that seven major OPEC+ producers will keep crude output unchanged in December, at September levels. Representatives from Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria and Oman met online to review the market.

The group raised output monthly from April through September and paused in October. The statement did not give a reason, but media reports say producers are still dealing with export disruptions caused by the Middle East conflict and will not adjust policy again before next year.

The longer agenda is capacity: each producer must complete a capacity assessment in the fourth quarter, which will set 2027 quotas. December’s decision closes this year’s output curve; next year’s quotas are the next negotiation.

Why it mattersHolding output steady does not add supply; it removes the expectation of more barrels. Monthly increases from April to September had already drawn down spare capacity, and pausing in October and December hands the price floor to spare capacity and the ceiling to geopolitics. For China, the crude-change rate behind domestic fuel-price adjustments becomes steadier, removing one variable from refinery crack spreads. For 2027, the fourth-quarter capacity review sets each country’s baseline and decides whether “cut to defend prices” survives. The risk is physical: if Middle East export disruptions widen, paper quotas and actual barrels diverge, and the geopolitical premium stops being something quotas can cap.
