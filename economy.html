看点这类生意的经济学很简单：把「减少还款」「拿回保费」「洗白征信」包装成可售卖的服务，收益归中介，成本留给金融机构、保险池和征信体系。平台封号只能切断获客入口，需求还在——真正的约束在两头：金融机构的催收与投诉核验流程，以及征信异议的免费渠道是否足够好用。对做金融内容与投放的团队，这是一次明确的合规边界：以「代理维权」名义收费，已经从灰色营销变成被点名的黑灰产。

经济日报给固态电池营销挤水分：最快路线只到 TRL5，规模化目标在 2030 年

经济日报 11 月 7 日刊文称，近期多家车企宣布固态电池量产下线，但中国科学院院士、清华大学教授欧阳明高直言：现在所有号称固态电池产业化的，都是固液混合电池，没有真正的全固态电池产业化。文章的核心区分在电解质——全固态不含任何液态成分，半固态仍是固态与液态的混合体，液态占比通常在 5% 至 10%。

产业化进度用技术成熟度衡量：固态电池三条路线中，进展最快的硫化物路线也只到 TRL5，即「相关环境中完成初步验证」，距离大规模量产还要走 TRL6 原型演示、TRL7 实际环境验证、TRL8 系统定型、TRL9 实际应用。工信部等七部门印发的《新型电池产业发展「十五五」规划》提出，到 2030 年全固态电池初步实现规模化应用。

文章还举了一个估值案例：芬兰初创公司 Donut Lab 在今年 CES 上发布「可量产」全固态电池，号称能量密度 400 瓦时/千克、约 5 分钟充电、10 万次循环后容量衰减小，估值一度超过 10 亿美元；几个月后被指所谓全固态不过是普通锂离子电池，芬兰金融监管与刑事侦查部门已介入。

看点「半固态」与「全固态」只差一个字，估值可以差一个数量级。判断一条量产新闻，先看三件事：电解质里还有没有液体、良率与成本是否披露、装车的是样品还是可交付批次。TRL5 到 TRL9 之间不是时间问题，是工程问题——界面阻抗、材料成本、规模化工艺，每一项都可能把一个季度拖成三年。对采购方，2030 年是规划目标，不是供货合同；对投资者，把「量产下线」当成「规模营收」是最贵的误读。

Two regulators target online financial black markets, naming debt-avoidance, insurance-refund and credit-repair accounts

On November 7, the National Financial Regulatory Administration said the Cyberspace Administration of China, working with it, had stepped up oversight of online financial information and shut down a batch of accounts promoting "malicious debt evasion", "improper anti-collection", "claims agency" and "credit repair" services. It published several typical cases.

Three playbooks were named. Accounts such as "Principal Zheng", "Principal's Breakout Circle" and "Just an Ordinary Person" sold anti-collection courses urging borrowers to avoid lawful debts. "Teacher Wu Refunds", "Teacher Li Dawei" and "Xiaolei Legal Consulting" advertised "full refunds" and "new refund rules", coaxing policyholders to invent insurer violations, file malicious complaints and pay high fees. "Self-Strengthening Brother Lao Xiang", "Apei Tells the Truth" and the "Minxing Micro-Academy" WeChat account fabricated successful credit-repair cases to sell paid services. The Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Xianyu, Kuaishou, Weibo and WeChat accounts involved were closed.

Why it mattersThe economics are simple: package "pay less", "get the premium back" or "clean the record" as a service, and the intermediary keeps the fee while the lender, the insurance pool and the credit system absorb the cost. Closing accounts cuts off customer acquisition, not demand. The real constraints sit at both ends — how lenders verify complaints, and whether the free credit-dispute channel is usable enough. For teams in financial content and advertising, this draws a clear line: charging for "agency rights defence" has moved from grey marketing to a named black market.

11/07 18:23 Economic Daily ↗

Economic Daily deflates solid-state battery hype: fastest route at TRL5, scale-up targeted for 2030

Economic Daily published an article on November 7 noting that several automakers had announced solid-state battery production lines, while Ouyang Minggao, an academician at the Chinese Academy of Sciences and professor at Tsinghua, said flatly that everything claimed as industrialised is a hybrid cell — no true all-solid-state battery has been industrialised. The distinction is the electrolyte: all-solid contains no liquid; semi-solid remains a mix, typically with 5–10% liquid electrolyte.

Progress is measured by technology readiness level. Of the three solid-state routes, the fastest — sulphide — sits at TRL5, "preliminary validation in a relevant environment". Mass production still requires TRL6 prototype demonstration, TRL7 real-environment validation, TRL8 system qualification and TRL9 actual deployment. A plan jointly issued by the industry ministry and six other departments targets initial scale application of all-solid-state cells by 2030.

The article also cites a valuation case: Finnish start-up Donut Lab unveiled a "production-ready" all-solid battery at this year's CES, claiming 400 Wh/kg, about five minutes of charging and limited degradation after 100,000 cycles; its valuation briefly exceeded $1bn. Months later it was alleged to be an ordinary lithium-ion battery, and Finnish financial regulators and criminal investigators stepped in.

Why it mattersOne word separates semi-solid from all-solid, and the valuation gap can be an order of magnitude. To judge a production claim, ask three things: is there still liquid in the electrolyte, are yield and cost disclosed, and is the cell a sample or a deliverable batch? The distance from TRL5 to TRL9 is an engineering problem, not a scheduling one — interface impedance, materials cost and scale-up processes can each turn a quarter into three years. For buyers, 2030 is a planning target, not a supply contract; for investors, treating "rolled off the line" as "scaled revenue" is the expensive mistake.
