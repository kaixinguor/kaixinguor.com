美国 10 月非农新增 16.5 万、失业率 4.1%，12 月加息概率升到 76.8%

美国劳工部 11 月 6 日发布数据，10 月非农就业岗位增加 16.5 万个，失业率为 4.1%，低于前月的 4.2%。这一增幅高于过去 12 个月 4.8 万个的月均水平。

劳工部同时上修前两个月：8 月由增加 13.3 万修正为 18.6 万，9 月由 2.9 万修正为 6 万，两月合计较此前公布数据多 8.4 万人。

就业增长集中在三块：专业和商业服务新增 7 万个（前月不足 1 万）、医疗保健 4.2 万个、交通运输和仓储 2.5 万个。

工资端，10 月私营部门非农就业人员平均时薪环比上涨 14 美分至 38 美元，同比上涨 3.1%。

截至记者发稿，芝加哥商品交易所 FedWatch 工具显示，市场预计美联储 12 月加息的概率为 76.8%，较一天前上升近 10 个百分点。今年 9 月美联储三年多来首次加息，10 月议息会维持利率不变。

看点这份数据把「12 月加不加」从政治不确定性改成了算术：时薪同比 3.1% 加失业率 4.1%，通往 2% 通胀的最后一段由工资决定，不由选举结果决定。成本落在 2027 年要重定价的浮动利率债务与按揭上；对新兴市场，76.8% 的加息概率意味着美元融资成本还要再抬一次，先动的是短端。
另一个视角对算力投资，时薪 38 美元、同比涨 3.1% 会同时抬高数据中心建设与运维的人工成本，让「用算力替代人力」的相对价格变得没那么有利；若 12 月真的加息，长久期项目的折现率再上一档，资本开支的回收期会被重新算一遍。

The US Labour Department said on November 6 that nonfarm payrolls rose by 165,000 in October and the unemployment rate was 4.1%, down from 4.2% a month earlier. The gain topped the 48,000 monthly average of the past 12 months.

Revisions went the same way: August was lifted to 186,000 from 133,000 and September to 60,000 from 29,000, adding 84,000 jobs across the two months.

Hiring was concentrated in professional and business services (+70,000, after less than 10,000 the month before), health care (+42,000) and transport and warehousing (+25,000).

Pay accelerated: average hourly earnings for private workers rose 14 cents to $38, up 3.1% from a year earlier.

By the time the report was filed, CME's FedWatch tool showed a 76.8% probability of a December hike, up nearly 10 percentage points in a day. The Fed raised rates in September for the first time in more than three years and held in October.

Why it mattersThe report turns December from a political question into arithmetic: with wages at 3.1% and unemployment at 4.1%, the last mile to 2% inflation is set by pay, not by election results. The cost lands on floating-rate debt and mortgages repricing in 2027; for emerging markets, a 76.8% hike probability means another step up in dollar funding costs, starting at the short end.
The other view For compute investment, $38 an hour and 3.1% wage growth raise both the build and the operating cost of data centres, weakening the case for replacing labour with machines. If the Fed does hike, long-duration projects get another notch on the discount rate and every capex payback gets recalculated.
