央行三季度报告：贷款定价基准要从单一参考 LPR 转向多元，DR001 前三季度均值 1.34%

中国人民银行 11 月 11 日发布《2026 年第三季度中国货币政策执行报告》。报告披露，前三季度国内生产总值同比增长 4.6%；银行体系流动性充裕，前三季度货币市场隔夜利率 DR001 均值为 1.34%；9 月末社会融资规模存量、广义货币供应量（M2）同比分别增长 7.1% 和 7.2%。

价格端，9 月新发放企业贷款利率略低于 3.0%，较上年同期低约 0.2 个百分点；新发放个人住房贷款利率约为 3.1%，与上年同期基本持平。报告称，在年初下调结构性货币政策工具利率的基础上，进一步下调抵押补充贷款（PSL）利率 0.25 个百分点，并持续推进明示企业贷款综合融资成本工作、降低融资中间费用。

结构端，科技创新和技术改造再贷款额度增加 2000 亿元、支持比例由 60% 上调至 100%；支农支小再贷款额度增加 5000 亿元，其中民营企业再贷款额度增加 3000 亿元；“六张网”建设纳入 PSL 支持范围。9 月末，科技、绿色、普惠、养老产业、数字经济产业贷款同比分别增长 12.0%、13.0%、6.5%、16.2%、15.7%，均高于全部贷款增速。

下一步取向，报告提出“稳妥有序推动货币政策操作框架改革完善，更好引导货币市场短端利率围绕政策利率平稳运行”，并推动“贷款利率定价基准从比较单一地参考贷款市场报价利率（LPR）定价向更加多元的定价基准转型”。汇率方面，9 月末人民币对美元汇率收盘价为 6.7053 元，较上年末升值 4.1%；CFETS 人民币汇率指数为 103.20，较上年末升值 5.3%。

看点把定价基准从“单一参考 LPR”改成“多元”，动的不是利率水平，而是传导链条：过去降 LPR 约等于降企业融资成本，之后要看政策利率、存款成本与基差怎么组合。9 月新发放企业贷款利率已略低于 3.0%、个人住房贷款利率约 3.1%，两者相差约 10 个基点，靠继续压贷款利率来刺激的空间已经很小；报告同时下调 PSL 利率 0.25 个百分点、把科创再贷款支持比例提到 100%，等于把“降价”换成了“定向补贴”。另一个口径要盯住：社融存量增 7.1%、M2 增 7.2%，都高于 4.6% 的实际 GDP 增速——债务的分母是名义增速，金融总量继续快于产出，杠杆率就还在往上走。
另一个视角对银行司库，基准多元化意味着 LPR 不再是唯一的报价锚，资产与负债两端的重定价缺口要靠内部资金转移定价重新切；对做债的机构，DR001 均值 1.34% 把短端波动区间框住，杠杆收益更多取决于资金面的确定性，而不是久期。

PBOC Q3 report: loan pricing to move beyond a single LPR reference as DR001 averages 1.34%

The People's Bank of China published its third-quarter 2026 monetary policy report on November 11. GDP grew 4.6% in the first nine months, the overnight money-market rate DR001 averaged 1.34%, and at end-September the stock of total social financing was 7.1% higher than a year earlier while M2 was up 7.2%.

On prices, new corporate loans were priced just under 3.0% in September, about 0.2 percentage point below a year earlier, and new mortgages averaged around 3.1%, roughly flat. The report said the PBOC cut the rate on pledged supplementary lending by 0.25 point, on top of January's cuts to structural tool rates, and kept pressing lenders to disclose all-in borrowing costs so that fees in the middle of the chain come down.

On structure, the relending quota for technology innovation and industrial upgrading was raised by Rmb200bn with the share of eligible costs covered lifted from 60% to 100%; the agricultural and small-business quota rose by Rmb500bn, including Rmb300bn earmarked for private firms; and construction of the "six networks" was added to PSL-eligible projects. At end-September, technology, green, inclusive, elderly-care and digital-economy loans were up 12.0%, 13.0%, 6.5%, 16.2% and 15.7% respectively, every one of them faster than total lending.

Looking ahead, the report pledged to "steadily reform the monetary policy operating framework" so that short-term money-market rates trade around the policy rate, and to shift loan pricing "from a relatively single reference to the loan prime rate toward a more diversified set of benchmarks". The yuan closed at 6.7053 per dollar at end-September, 4.1% stronger than a year earlier, and the CFETS basket index stood at 103.20, up 5.3%.

Why it mattersChanging the benchmark changes the transmission chain, not the level of rates. Cutting LPR used to be shorthand for cheaper corporate credit; from here the outcome depends on the policy rate, deposit costs and the spread lenders add. With corporate loans just under 3.0% and mortgages near 3.1%, the gap is only about 10bp, so there is little room left to squeeze loan rates; instead the report cut PSL by 0.25 point and doubled the coverage ratio on tech relending — a subsidy replaces a price cut. One denominator deserves attention: social financing at 7.1% and M2 at 7.2% both outrun real GDP growth of 4.6%, and debt is measured against nominal output.
The other viewFor a bank treasurer, a multi-benchmark regime means LPR stops being the only anchor and the repricing gap has to be managed through internal funds transfer pricing; for a bond fund, a 1.34% DR001 average caps short-rate volatility, so carry depends on certainty of funding rather than duration.

11/11 16:20 Ministry of Finance ↗

63-day T-bill clears at 1.05% as the MOF sells Rmb40.61bn against a Rmb40bn plan

The Ministry of Finance said in announcement No. 193 of 2026, published on November 11, that the 73rd discount T-bill of the year had been auctioned: Rmb40bn was planned and Rmb40.61bn of face value was actually issued.

The bill runs 63 days and was sold at 99.819 per 100 of face value, equivalent to an annualised yield of 1.05%. Interest starts accruing on November 12, distribution runs through the same day, trading begins on November 16, and the bill is repaid at par on January 14, 2027, or the next business day.

The same morning the PBOC lent overnight cash at 1.25%, and its quarterly report put the DR001 average for the first nine months at 1.34%.

Why it mattersA Rmb40bn plan that drew Rmb40.61bn, 1.5% more, shows solid demand for two-month safety.
