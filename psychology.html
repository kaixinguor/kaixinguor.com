11/24 Journal of Gambling Studies（论文） ↗

4,375 名美国大学生：6% 过去一年在线赌球，配对后只剩「酒精后果更多」一项差异

《Journal of Gambling Studies》（《赌博研究杂志》）2026 年 11 月 24 日在线发表（DOI 10.1007/s10899-026-10579-z）。第一作者 Fung HL、通讯作者 Holt LJ，单位是康涅狄格州三一学院（Trinity College）心理学系；第三作者 Herchenroeder L 来自美国海军学院领导力、伦理与法律系。

设计是横断面在线调查，样本来自美国六所高校，N=4,375，平均年龄 19.34 岁。核心结局是过去一年是否在线赌球（OSB）；同时测社会经济人口学特征、下注动机与精神症状。4,375 人中有 274 人（6%）报告过去一年 OSB——作者在引言里强调，在线体育博彩在美国是一种相对年轻的赌博形式，而新兴成年人是参与率最高的人群，这正是这篇要补的第一个空白。

谁在赌：拉丁裔（相对白人）、参加俱乐部或校内联赛的运动员、加入希腊字母社团（fraternity/sorority）的学生，过去一年 OSB 的比例更高；女性、亚裔、性少数身份则与更低的概率相关。这些都是相关，不是原因——横断面设计一次测量，方向无法判定。

最值得看的是配对那一步。未配对时，不赌球的学生抑郁、焦虑、ADHD 症状更高，酒精后果更低——这与「赌球者心理更差」的直觉相反。研究者按性别、年级与性取向把两组配对后，唯一剩下的差异是赌球组的酒精后果更多。在赌球者内部，「对自己下注有保留」（amotivation）预测更多赌球相关问题；抑郁、ADHD 症状、自伤念头与酒精后果都和赌球问题正相关。若你或身边的人有自伤念头：中国大陆可拨打全国统一心理援助热线 12356，美国可拨打 988。

口径：单一时点、自报、便利样本，六所高校不能代表全美；结局是「有没有赌」与「赌球问题多少」，不是临床诊断；配对只能平衡已测的协变量，平衡不了未测的。6% 是自报的过去一年比例，实际值可能被低估。

看点这条真正的贡献不是「大学生在赌球」，而是它把「精神症状与赌博共存」这个结论拆开检验了一遍：不配对时，症状差异指向赌球者；配平性别、年级与性取向后，症状差异消失，只剩酒精。两种读法给校园干预的方向完全不同——前者指向筛查抑郁焦虑，后者指向把赌球与饮酒放在同一张问卷里。证据强度：六校、4,375 人、有配对设计，在赌博研究里算扎实；但横断面、自报、非概率抽样，且 6% 的暴露组只有 274 人，分组后每一格的样本都不大。成本落在谁身上：校园预防项目的预算通常按「病种」切分，赌博与酒精分属两个口径，而数据说它们在同一批人身上叠加；把两项合并筛查，边际成本几乎为零。
另一个视角 274 人里「对下注有保留」这一项预测了更多问题，这意味着干预窗口不在「赌不赌」，而在「第一次觉得不对劲」——那一句自我报告比任何症状量表都便宜。

11/24 Obesity Reviews（论文） ↗

18 项社交媒体饮食干预的系统综述：13 项做了显著性检验，8 项至少改善一种饮食行为

《Obesity Reviews》（《肥胖评论》）2026 年 11 月 24 日在线发表（DOI 10.1111/obr.70264），文章号 e70264。作者 Tang H、Spreckley M、Tuck M、van Sluijs EM、Ahern AL、Smith AD，单位是剑桥大学代谢科学流行病学单元与剑桥医学院。

设计是混合方法系统综述：检索 7 个电子数据库至 2025 年 2 月，纳入面向 13–35 岁人群、评估社交媒体干预对饮食行为与膳食质量影响的研究；质量评价用混合方法评价工具（MMAT），由三名评审独立完成；资料提取双人重复，量化与质性结果用聚敛分离式（convergent segregated）路径整合。

结果：16 篇文献、18 项干预，其中 12 项为定量设计、4 项为混合方法。干预主要瞄准三类行为——增加蔬果摄入、减少含糖饮料、减少零食。在 13 项（来自 11 项研究）做了显著性检验的干预里，8 项至少在一个饮食行为上出现正向变化。这个比例说明「有信号」，但也说明近四成没有检出变化。

质性合成给出六个主题：真人故事带来联结与社群感；具体可执行的建议才推动改变；来源可信是前提；整体健康叙事比单点恐吓更受欢迎；幽默提升参与；隐私与可用性问题阻碍长期参与。换句话说，这类干预的失效点通常不在内容，而在「能不能一直用下去」。

口径：纳入研究的随访普遍偏短，饮食结局多为自报，干预剂量、平台与对照形态差异大，无法合并出统一效应量；MMAT 评级不等于证据质量分级；13–35 岁的跨度把青少年与成年早期混在一起，两者的平台使用习惯并不相同。

看点把「社交媒体能不能改善饮食」换成一个可数的问题：18 项干预里只有 13 项做了显著性检验，其中 8 项动了至少一个饮食行为，5 项没有。这个 8/13 的分母比任何单个「有效」结论都更有用——它说明这类干预的效应不稳定，且不稳定的部分与设计有关（有没有对照、随访多长、结局是否自报）。证据强度：系统综述加 MMAT 评级在观察性证据里偏硬，但受限于原始研究的质量，最终只能给出方向。成本落在公共卫生预算：一次社交媒体推广的单位成本远低于面对面营养咨询，可如果长期参与被隐私与可用性卡住，实际到达剂量会远低于设计剂量——按「曝光」付费与按「行为改变」付费，是两本账。

11/24 Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology（论文） ↗

53,931 名挪威难民背景人口：融入计划期间用过心理卫生服务，一年后就业或就学概率更低

《Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology》（《社会精神病学与精神流行病学》）2026 年 11 月 24 日在线发表（DOI 10.1007/s00127-026-03252-8）。第一作者 Straiton ML、通讯作者同为 Straiton ML，单位是挪威公共卫生研究所精神健康系；合作者来自挪威暴力与创伤应激研究中心、NORCE 研究机构。

设计是登记数据链接研究：纳入 2008–2019 年间参加挪威引入计划（Norwegian Introduction Programme，NIP）的 53,931 名难民背景人口，用改良泊松回归加稳健标准误估计「计划结束后一个日历年处于就业或就学」的风险比，暴露变量是 NIP 期间初级与二级心理卫生服务的使用。

结果的方向很明确：NIP 期间使用过心理卫生服务——尤其是二级服务——与一年后处于就业或就学的概率更低相关。这个关联在女性中更弱，并随来源国不同而变化；作者同时给出了校正后的预测概率。

口径：登记数据能覆盖全人群、几乎没有失访，这是它比问卷强的地方；但「使用服务」不等于「有需求」，也不等于严重程度——能建档、能转诊本身就受语言、信息与地区供给影响。就业与就学是登记口径的二分类结局，无法区分兼职、短时与不稳定就业；观察性设计也无法排除「症状更重的人本来就更难进入劳动力市场」这一条通路，甚至可能就是主因。

看点这条的政策含义与它的因果强度不成正比：如果「用过心理服务」是就业劣势的标记，那么把心理服务当成融入计划的配套，反而可能把最需要支持的人先标出来；如果它是原因（治疗中断、污名、时间成本），那么改善的抓手在服务组织而不是个人。证据强度：五万余人、十一年、全国登记链接，样本量与失访控制都硬；软处是暴露与结局都来自行政记录，严重度无法测量，效应量随性别与来源国漂移，说明「平均效应」在这里几乎没有意义。成本落在谁身上：NIP 是带补贴的全国性融入项目，参与者的一年窗口很短；把心理服务安排在白天、与语言课程冲突，等于让最需要的人二选一——这不是疗效问题，是排班问题。

11/24 Journal of Sleep Research（论文） ↗

819 名澳大利亚见习心理学家：一次免费线上睡眠工作坊后，CBT-I 知识与自我效能全部 p<0.001

《Journal of Sleep Research》（《睡眠研究杂志》）2026 年 11 月 24 日在线发表（DOI 10.1111/jsr.70535），文章号 e70535。第一作者 Meaklim H，单位是弗林德斯大学健康与医学研究院睡眠健康旗舰项目、奥斯汀健康呼吸与睡眠研究所与墨尔本大学心理科学学院；合作者来自澳大利亚睡眠协会、西澳大学、睡眠健康基金会、莫纳什大学 Turner 脑与心理健康研究所等。

问题背景：失眠的认知行为治疗（cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia，CBT-I）是失眠的一线治疗，却很少出现在心理学研究生培养里，这直接限制了它的可及性。研究用 RE-AIM 框架评估 2024 年澳大利亚睡眠协会面向全国见习心理学家开设的免费线上工作坊——对象包括硕士、博士与实习路径中的注册者。

覆盖：工作坊触达当年在澳大利亚心理师注册委员会注册的见习心理学家总数的 10.3%，即 n=819 人。评估为前后测设计，测量睡眠与 CBT-I 知识、管理睡眠困扰的自我效能，以及参加者自身的睡眠健康。

结果：三项指标全部显著改善（所有 p<0.001）；88% 的参加者把工作坊整体评为「很好」或「优秀」。作者同时指出，要把 CBT-I 真正推进心理实践，还需要持续与扩大的睡眠教育，而不是一次工作坊。

口径：没有对照组，前后测的变化无法与「重复测量」「选择偏差」分开——愿意报名的人本来就更关心睡眠；结局全部自报，知识测验只能测「知道」，测不到「在诊室里做」；10.3% 是注册人群的覆盖率，不是实践改变率。RE-AIM 的「采纳」与「维持」两个维度，这篇只碰到了边缘。

看点这条的价值在分母：CBT-I 的供给瓶颈通常被描述为「治疗师不够」，而这里给出的是更具体的一段——培养管道里没有这门课。819 人、10.3%、一次免费线上工作坊，就把知识、自我效能与个人睡眠三项同时推动，单位成本极低；但「知道」与「开展」之间还有督导、收费编码与转诊路径三道门。证据强度：全国覆盖、明确分母、前后测全部 p<0.001 是很干净的投送证据；没有对照、自报结局、自选样本，使它只能证明「可以做得到」，不能证明「做了就有效」。成本落在谁身上：一次线上工作坊的价格约等于一场会议的分会场，而 CBT-I 在多数医保体系里仍缺少独立付费编码——不解决付费，培训出来的供给不会自动变成服务。

11/24 JMIR Aging（论文） ↗

13,179 条 Reddit 帖由本地开源大模型编码：照护负担均值从早期 5.38 升到晚期 7.77

《JMIR Aging》2026 年 11 月 24 日在线发表（DOI 10.2196/103305），文章号 e103305。作者 Zhang Y、Zhang M，单位是德州州立大学（Texas State University）。研究处理 2020 年 1 月至 2025 年 6 月的 Reddit 月度存档，从 5 个阿尔茨海默病及相关痴呆（ADRD）子版与 4 个更宽的照护/老龄化子版中抽取，构成 13,200 帖的平衡语料。

方法的核心是「把帖子变成变量」：一个本地部署的开源权重模型为每帖推断结构化字段——照护者人口学、角色、ADRD 分期、照护场所、支持需求、主导情绪、情感倾向、负担分与主要/次要压力源；流程包含模式校验、不确定性评级、字段级纠错提示、来源匹配，并用 150 帖双人编码做人工基准，最后做配对亚组分析。来源匹配后的分析样本为 13,179 帖。

信度：在照护者角色、ADRD 分期、照护场所、主导情绪、支持需求类型、负担分组与压力源类别上，人与模型的吻合度为 Cohen κ=0.77–1.00；但在最初的「是否在范围内」筛选上，κ=0.00——这一步基本失效，说明流水线里最脆弱的是入口判断，不是字段抽取。

结果：成年子女是最大的照护者群体（8,266/13,179，62.7%），孙辈 2,012 人（15.3%）；中度 ADRD 为最常见分期（6,554，49.7%），居家照护为最常见场所（7,145，54.2%）。负担均值随分期上升：早期 5.38（标准差 2.47）、中度 6.96（2.17）、晚期 7.77（2.16）。行为性压力是最主要的一级压力源，家庭冲突、经济压力、情绪耗竭与社会隔离是主要二级压力源。高负担亚组集中在「晚期＋情绪支持需求」（均值 8.42）、「照护转衔＋情绪支持需求」（8.45）与「晚期照护转衔」（8.40）。

口径：语料是 Reddit 用户的自述，既不代表全部照护者，也无法核实；「负担分」由模型从文本推断，量纲不是任何临床量表；κ=0.77–1.00 只说明与 150 帖人工编码一致，基准本身很小；平台用户偏年轻、偏英语、偏愿意公开叙述。

看点这条最该被记住的是那个 κ=0.00：一条 LLM 流水线可以在七个字段上达到 0.77 以上，却在「这篇帖子算不算范围内」这一步完全失效——而入口判断决定了后面所有比例的分母。把它当成「AI 能读懂照护者」的证据会误读；把它当成「LLM 可以替代人工编码，但必须逐段验证」的案例才准确。证据强度：13,179 帖、明确的人工基准、公开的字段定义，在计算社会科学里属于可复现的做法；但基准只有 150 帖、语料单一平台、负担分无外部效标。成本落在谁身上：人工编码一万三千帖需要数月人力，本地模型几小时完成，省下的是编码成本而不是研究设计成本；真正的支出转移到验证环节——150 帖双人编码、字段级纠错与不确定性评级，才是这套流水线贵的地方。

11/24 Assessment（论文） ↗

460 人的随机试验：把手机即时评估的采样频率调高，没有制造测量反应性

《Assessment》（《评估》）2026 年 11 月 24 日在线发表（DOI 10.1177/10731911261503855）。第一作者 Jones DR，单位是犹他大学与 Huntsman 癌症研究所；合作者来自俄克拉何马大学健康科学中心。研究把 460 名参与者（77% 女性）随机分配到不同的手机即时评估（ecological momentary assessment，EMA）频率。

要检验的是一个长期争议：频繁追问「你现在压力多大」会不会改变被追问的人——测量反应性（measurement reactivity）的常见机制包括自我增强动机、练习效应与疲劳。若反应性成立，采样越密，报告的压力与情绪应该被系统性推偏。

结果与反应性预期不一致：更频繁的 EMA 与更低的平均压力、更低的愤怒、更高的幸福感相关，但效应形态更像「数据更多、测量更精确」，而不是「被测量改变了」。评估频率对健康行为结局没有实质影响；虽然在部分模型里频率调节了情绪/压力与健康行为之间的关联，但效应量微不足道。

口径：单一样本、自报结局、随访期有限；「更密」与「更稀」的具体间隔由设计决定，不能外推到极端频率（例如每天数十次）；77% 女性意味着性别分布偏斜；「没有实质影响」是基于效应量判断，而不是等价性检验，因此不能写成「证明没有影响」。

看点这条改变的是设计成本核算：如果提高采样密度不会污染数据，那么研究者在「精度」与「负担」之间的取舍就可以向前者倾斜，而不必为了「别打扰太多次」牺牲时间分辨率。证据强度：随机分配频率是这类问题里最干净的设计，N=460 也够用；但结局自报、单一平台、随访短，结论限于「按天数次」的量级。成本落在谁身上：EMA 的真正成本是参与者的依从性与脱落，频率越高，脱落风险越大——这篇测了偏差，没有测脱落；把「无反应性」当成「可以随便加次数」，会在另一个变量上付账。

11/24 Journal of Gambling Studies (paper) ↗

4,375 US undergraduates: 6 per cent bet on sports online last year, and matching leaves only heavier alcohol consequences

The Journal of Gambling Studies published the study online on November 24, 2026 (DOI 10.1007/s10899-026-10579-z). Fung HL is first author and Holt LJ corresponding author, both in the Department of Psychology at Trinity College in Hartford, Connecticut; Herchenroeder L, the third author, is in the Department of Leadership, Ethics and Law at the United States Naval Academy.

The design is a cross-sectional online survey drawn from six US institutions, N = 4,375, mean age 19.34. The outcome was past-year online sports betting (OSB), measured alongside sociodemographic characteristics, betting motivations and psychiatric symptoms. Of those 4,375 students, 274 (6 per cent) reported betting on sports online in the past year — a headline number for what the authors call a relatively new form of gambling in the United States, and one that emerging adults are the likeliest to take up.

Who bets: Latinx students relative to White students, club or intramural athletes relative to non-athletes, and students in Greek life all showed higher past-year OSB. Female sex, Asian race and sexual-minority identity were associated with lower odds. These are associations from a single measurement, so direction cannot be settled.

The matching step is the interesting one. Before matching, students who did not bet reported higher depressive, anxiety and ADHD symptoms and lower alcohol consequences — the opposite of the intuitive story. After matching on sex, class year and sexual orientation, the only remaining difference was that the betting group reported more alcohol consequences. Within the betting group, having reservations about one's betting — amotivation — predicted more betting-related problems, and depression, ADHD symptoms, thoughts of self-harm and alcohol consequences all correlated positively with those problems. If you or someone close to you is having thoughts of self-harm, China's national psychological assistance hotline is 12356 and the US line is 988.

Limits: one time point, self-report, a convenience sample, and six institutions that do not represent US higher education. The outcome is whether students bet and how many problems they report, not a clinical diagnosis, and matching balances only measured covariates. Six per cent is a self-reported past-year figure and is more likely to understate than overstate.

Why it mattersThe contribution is not that students bet, but that the study tested the coexistence story twice. Unmatched, symptom differences point at the bettors; matched on sex, class year and sexual orientation, those differences disappear and alcohol is all that remains. The two readings send campus prevention in opposite directions — one towards depression and anxiety screening, the other towards putting betting and drinking on the same form. On evidence, six campuses, 4,375 students and a matching design are solid for gambling research; cross-sectional self-report from a non-probability sample, with only 274 exposed students, means the cells get thin fast. The cost lands on budgets: campus prevention money is usually allocated by condition, and gambling and alcohol sit in separate lines even though the data put them in the same students. Screening for both together costs almost nothing at the margin.
Another angle Among those 274, having reservations about one's own betting predicted more problems, which places the intervention window not at whether someone bets but at the first moment they feel uneasy — and that single self-report is cheaper than any symptom scale.

11/24 Obesity Reviews (paper) ↗

Eighteen social-media diet interventions: 13 were tested for significance, eight moved at least one eating behaviour

Obesity Reviews published the review online on November 24, 2026 (DOI 10.1111/obr.70264), article e70264. Tang H, Spreckley M, Tuck M, van Sluijs EM, Ahern AL and Smith AD wrote it, at the MRC Epidemiology Unit and the School of Clinical Medicine at the University of Cambridge.

The design is a mixed-methods systematic review: seven electronic databases searched to February 2025, including studies that assessed changes in eating behaviour and dietary quality after social-media interventions in people aged 13 to 35. Quality was appraised independently by three reviewers with the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool, data were extracted in duplicate, and quantitative and qualitative findings were integrated through a convergent segregated approach.

Results: 16 articles reporting 18 interventions, 12 quantitative and four mixed-methods. The interventions targeted three behaviours mainly — more fruit and vegetables, less sugar-sweetened beverage, less snacking. Of the 13 interventions (from 11 studies) that were evaluated with significance testing, eight produced positive change in at least one dietary behaviour. That is a signal, and also a reminder that close to four in ten detected nothing.

The qualitative synthesis produced six themes: personal stories build connection and community; actionable, practical tips support change; trust in credible sources is essential; holistic well-being messaging is preferred; humour raises engagement; and privacy and usability concerns block sustained participation. The failure point for these interventions is usually not the content but whether anyone keeps using it.

Limits: follow-up in the included studies was generally short, dietary outcomes were mostly self-reported, and interventions varied too much in dose, platform and comparator to pool into one effect size. An MMAT rating is not a certainty grade, and a 13-to-35 age span merges adolescents with young adults whose platform habits differ.

Why it mattersThe review turns “can social media improve diet” into something countable: of 18 interventions, only 13 were tested for significance — eight moved at least one eating behaviour and five did not. That 8-in-13 denominator is more useful than any single positive finding, because it says the effect is unstable and that the instability tracks design — whether there was a comparator, how long follow-up ran, whether outcomes were self-reported. On evidence, a systematic review with MMAT appraisal is hard currency among observational designs, but the underlying studies cap how far it can go. The cost sits in public-health budgets: a social-media campaign is far cheaper per person than face-to-face nutrition counselling, but if sustained engagement is blocked by privacy and usability, the dose that actually arrives is well below the dose designed — and paying per impression is a different ledger from paying per behaviour change.

The design links national registries: 53,931 people with refugee backgrounds who took part in the Norwegian Introduction Programme (NIP) between 2008 and 2019. The direction is unambiguous: using mental health services during the NIP — secondary care in particular — was associated with a lower probability of being in education or employment a year after completion. The association was weaker among women and varied by country of origin, and the authors also report adjusted predicted probabilities.

Limits: registries cover the whole population with almost no loss to follow-up, which is what makes them stronger than surveys, but “used a service” is not the same as “had a need” and says nothing about severity — getting a record opened and a referral made depends on language, information and local supply. Education and employment are binary registry outcomes that cannot separate part-time, short-term or unstable work, and an observational design cannot rule out the pathway where more severe symptoms make labour-market entry harder in the first place, which may be the main one.

Why it mattersThe policy reading here is larger than the causal strength. If service use is a marker of disadvantage, then attaching mental health care to an integration programme may flag the people who most need support; if it is a cause — interrupted treatment, stigma, time cost — then the lever sits in service organisation rather than in individuals. On evidence, over fifty thousand people, eleven years and linked national registries are hard to beat on size and attrition; the soft part is that exposure and outcome both come from administrative records, severity is unmeasured, and the effect drifts by sex and origin, which means an average effect has little meaning here. The cost falls on participants: the NIP is a subsidised programme with a short one-year horizon, and scheduling appointments during the day, on top of language classes, asks the people who need care most to choose between the two. That is a timetabling problem, not an efficacy problem.

11/24 Journal of Sleep Research (paper) ↗

819 Australian provisional psychologists: one free online workshop moved CBT-I knowledge and self-efficacy at p < .001

Journal of Sleep Research published the evaluation online on November 24, 2026 (DOI 10.1111/jsr.70535), article e70535. Meaklim H is first author, at the Flinders Health and Medical Research Institute Sleep Health Flagship, the Institute for Breathing and Sleep at Austin Health and the Melbourne School of Psychological Sciences; co-authors come from the Australasian Sleep Association, the University of Western Australia, the Sleep Health Foundation, Monash University's Turner Institute and elsewhere.

The problem is a pipeline one: cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) is the gold-standard treatment for insomnia and is rarely taught in graduate psychology pathways, which caps how far it reaches. Guided by the RE-AIM framework, the study evaluated a free online sleep workshop that the Australasian Sleep Association offered in 2024 to every provisional psychologist in Australia — master's, doctorate and internship pathways alike.

Reach: the workshop drew 10.3 per cent of everyone registered with the Psychology Board of Australia as a provisional psychologist that year, n = 819. Pre-post assessments covered sleep and CBT-I knowledge, self-efficacy for managing sleep disturbance, and participants' own sleep health.

Results: all three improved significantly, every p < .001, and 88 per cent rated the workshop overall as very good or excellent. The authors are explicit that spreading CBT-I into practice needs continued and expanded sleep education rather than a single workshop.

Limits: no control group, so change cannot be separated from repeated testing or selection — people who sign up for a sleep workshop already care about sleep. All outcomes are self-reported, and a knowledge test measures knowing, not doing in the consulting room. The 10.3 per cent figure is reach across the registered population, not a rate of practice change; of the RE-AIM dimensions, adoption and maintenance are barely touched.

Why it mattersThe value is in the denominator. The CBT-I supply problem is usually described as too few therapists, and this puts a number on a more specific segment: the training pipeline does not teach it. Eight hundred and nineteen people, 10.3 per cent of the national register, one free online workshop, and knowledge, self-efficacy and personal sleep all move together at very low unit cost. Between knowing and doing, though, sit supervision, billing codes and referral pathways. On evidence, national reach, a clear denominator and p < .001 across the board make this clean delivery evidence; no control, self-report and self-selection mean it shows the thing can be done, not that doing it works. The cost is roughly a conference breakout room per workshop, while CBT-I still lacks a stand-alone billing code in most insurance systems — and without payment, trained supply does not turn into services.

11/24 JMIR Aging (paper) ↗

13,179 Reddit posts coded by a locally hosted open-weight model: caregiver burden rises from 5.38 to 7.77 across dementia stages

JMIR Aging published the study online on November 24, 2026 (DOI 10.2196/103305), article e103305. Zhang Y and Zhang M, at Texas State University, processed monthly Reddit submission archives from January 2020 to June 2025, drawing posts from five dementia-specific subreddits and four broader caregiving or ageing ones to build a balanced 13,200-post corpus.

The core move is turning posts into variables. A locally hosted open-weight model inferred structured post-level fields — caregiver demographics, role, dementia stage, care setting, support need, dominant emotion, sentiment, burden and primary and secondary stressors. The workflow added schema validation, uncertainty ratings, field-level correction prompts, source matching and a 150-post double-coded human benchmark, then ran pairwise subgroup analyses. The source-matched analytic sample was 13,179 posts.

Reliability: human agreement was high for caregiver role, dementia stage, care setting, dominant emotion, support need type, burden group and stressor category (Cohen κ = 0.77 to 1.00), but poor for the initial in-scope screen (κ = 0.00). The most fragile step in the pipeline was the front door, not the field extraction.

Findings: adult children were the largest inferred caregiver group (8,266 of 13,179, 62.7 per cent), followed by grandchildren (2,012, 15.3 per cent). Moderate dementia was the modal stage (6,554, 49.7 per cent) and home the modal setting (7,145, 54.2 per cent). Mean burden rose with stage — 5.38 (SD 2.47) at early, 6.96 (2.17) at moderate, 7.77 (2.16) at late. Behavioural strain was the dominant primary stressor, with family conflict, financial strain, emotional strain and social isolation the leading secondary stressors. High-burden subgroups clustered around late-stage dementia with emotional support needs (mean 8.42), care transitions with emotional support needs (8.45) and late-stage care transitions (8.40).

Limits: Reddit posts are self-narratives that neither represent all caregivers nor can be verified; the burden score is inferred from text and is not on any clinical scale; κ of 0.77 to 1.00 means agreement with a 150-post benchmark, which is small; and platform users skew younger, English-speaking and willing to narrate in public.

Why it mattersThe number to carry away is that κ = 0.00. A model pipeline can reach 0.77 or better on seven fields and fail completely on whether a post belongs in scope — and the front door decides the denominator for everything downstream. Read as proof that AI understands caregivers, this misleads; read as a case that LLMs can stand in for human coding only with section-by-section validation, it is accurate. On evidence, 13,179 posts, a defined human benchmark and published field definitions make this reproducible by computational-social-science standards; the benchmark is 150 posts, the corpus is one platform, and the burden score has no external criterion. The cost lands in verification, not computation: hand-coding thirteen thousand posts takes months, a local model takes hours, and the expensive part of this pipeline is the double coding, field-level correction and uncertainty rating that make the output usable.

11/24 Assessment (paper) ↗

A 460-person randomised trial: asking smartphone survey questions more often bought precision, not reactivity

Assessment published the study online on November 24, 2026 (DOI 10.1177/10731911261503855). Jones DR is first author, at the University of Utah and the Huntsman Cancer Institute, with co-authors at the University of Oklahoma Health Sciences Center. The team randomised 460 participants (77 per cent female) to different frequencies of smartphone-based ecological momentary assessment (EMA).

The target was a long-running worry: does asking “how stressed are you right now” often enough change the person being asked? The usual mechanisms for measurement reactivity are self-enhancement motives, practice effects and fatigue, and if reactivity is real, denser sampling should push reported stress and mood systematically off course.

The results did not fit the reactivity story. More frequent EMA was associated with lower mean stress, lower anger and higher happiness, but the pattern looked like more data buying more precision rather than measurement changing the thing measured. Assessment frequency had no substantive effect on health behaviour outcomes, and although frequency sometimes moderated the links between affect or stress and health behaviour, those effect sizes were trivial.

Limits: one sample, self-reported outcomes, limited follow-up. The “denser” and “sparser” arms were set by the design and do not generalise to extreme schedules such as dozens of prompts a day; 77 per cent female is a skewed distribution; and “no substantive effect” is a judgement about effect size rather than an equivalence test, so it cannot be written up as proof of no effect.

Why it mattersThis changes the design arithmetic. If denser sampling does not contaminate the data, researchers can lean towards precision instead of sacrificing time resolution to avoid bothering people too often. On evidence, randomising frequency is the cleanest design available for this question and 460 people is enough; self-report, one platform and short follow-up confine the claim to a few prompts per day. The cost sits on the participant side: the real expense of EMA is compliance and dropout, and dropout rises with frequency. This study measured bias and left attrition unmeasured, so reading “no reactivity” as licence to add prompts freely would simply move the bill to another line.
