11/18 Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health（EurekAlert） ↗

138 项研究、35,296 名美加大学生：「不容忍不确定性」每年升 0.65 分，2012 年后升速快了三倍

这项研究发表在《JAMA Psychiatry》，哥伦比亚大学梅尔曼公共卫生学院 2026 年 11 月 18 日发布。第一作者 Jeroen M. Van Baar 是波士顿大学公共卫生学院流行病学博士后，资深作者 Katherine M. Keyes 是哥伦比亚梅尔曼流行病学教授，合作者包括里贾纳大学心理创伤与应激系统实验室的 R. Nicholas Carleton。研究由美国国家精神卫生研究所（NIMH）T32MH013043 资助，作者声明没有经济利益冲突。

做法是把几十年的研究拼成一条时间线。团队在 Web of Science 检索 2007 至 2025 年间发表、以美国和加拿大大学生为对象的同行评议研究，筛了约 300 篇全文，最终纳入 138 项研究、35,296 名完成「不容忍不确定性量表」（IUS-27 或 IUS-12）的参与者，按调查年份对平均分做回归。

结果是：1992 到 2023 年，平均分每年升约 0.65 分；转折点在 2012 年前后，2012 至 2023 年的升速约为 1992 至 2011 年的三倍。用作者的话说，IU 不是健康结局，也不是疾病的根因，而是「参与焦虑发生的一个中间认知过程」——比如非得知道课堂上会发生什么才敢走进教室，不知道就难受。

三处口径要一并读。第一，这不是同一批人被随访 31 年，而是不同年份、不同样本的横断面研究叠出的趋势，群体层面的变化不能直接读成「现在这一届学生更脆弱」。第二，IUS-27 与 IUS-12 两个版本混在一起分析，量表分数跨研究可比的假设本身就在结论里。第三，作者专门回应了「是不是只是心理健康意识提高、大家更愿意报告」：IU 这个概念在心理学之外几乎没有流行度，不容易被流行词带着走，所以这条上升不太可能只由报告习惯解释——但这是论证，不是证据。

真正能落地的部分在干预：针对不确定性的认知行为干预已经有效，如果趋势是真的，「不容忍不确定性」这个靶点比笼统的「焦虑」更具体，也更可测。

看点这条改变的不是「年轻人更焦虑了」这个结论，而是那场「是不是只是意识提高」的争论的举证方向。人群层面的上升通常有两种解释——真实变化，或者报告门槛降低；这项研究挑了一个在心理学圈外几乎没人用的构念来测，正是为了让第二种解释更难成立。证据强度要分层：138 项研究、3.5 万人、31 年跨度，比任何单点调查都稳；但它整合的是横断面数据，回答不了「同一批人是不是真的在变」，也排除不了同期发生的智能手机、学业竞争、经济不确定——作者自己只把它们列为「可能交织、互相强化」。要判断它能否改写筛查，看两件事：IUS 分数能不能预测日后的焦虑障碍发病（需要纵向队列），以及针对 IU 的干预能不能在随机试验里把发病压下去。
另一个视角把 0.65 分/年换算成尺度感：按这个速率线性外推，31 年累计约 20 分，而 IUS-27 是 27 道题、每题 1 到 5 分——两代人在同一张表上的差距，已经不是「一两道题答得不一样」的量级。

11/18 Uppsala University（EurekAlert） ↗

21 位瑞典新手父亲的访谈：孩子吃得少时最没底，他们要的是「这种情况该怎么办」

乌普萨拉大学 2026 年 11 月 18 日发布，论文发表在《PLOS One》。第一作者 Ylva Törner Le Priol 是该校食品研究、营养与饮食学系博士生，研究父亲在幼儿食物与进食中的角色；此前这一领域的研究对象大多是母亲。

方法是最朴素的一种：访谈 21 位瑞典新手父亲，孩子年龄在 10 到 24 个月之间——正好是从以奶为主过渡到家庭餐、挑食开始出现的窗口。

访谈里反复出现的是一条平衡线：一边让孩子自己探索食物，一边确保他吃够了。孩子吃得比预期少、或者对食物没什么兴趣时，父亲不确定该不该再劝一口、劝到哪一步算强迫。孩子体重较轻、增重较慢的父亲，这种不确定更明显；孩子较重的父亲没有表达同等程度的担忧。

他们要的不是理论，是「这种情况该怎么办」的具体答案：孩子扭头不吃时是把盘子收走还是再试一次、吃多少才算够。作者把落点放在儿童保健中心——孩子越大，家庭与保健机构的接触越少，而这恰好是很多父亲在育儿假里承担更多喂养责任的时候。

口径：质性访谈、21 人、单一国家、父亲自报，没有测量孩子的实际摄入量，也没有对照组。它能给出问题清单和假设，给不出比例，更不能说明父亲的不确定造成了喂养问题。

看点这类研究在版面上常被写成「父亲参与不足」，但它真正指出的是服务设计的一处错位：儿童保健的接触频率随月龄下降，而喂养的不确定性在 10 至 24 个月这个窗口上升——两条曲线是反着走的。对做儿保的机构，可操作的改动不是再发一张辅食添加表，而是在 12 个月和 18 个月这两次随访里加一个具体问题：「孩子不吃的时候你怎么办」，再按父亲的回答给脚本。证据上要诚实：21 人的访谈只能提出问题，证明不了改变咨询方式会改善孩子的饮食；要证明，得把「父亲版喂养咨询」做成对照试验，结局至少包括实际摄入与生长曲线，而不只是父亲自评的信心。

英格兰 73 家信托里 41 家仍在走廊收治高危病人：精神危机患者与 15 岁以下儿童都在名单上

《BMJ》2026 年 11 月 18 日发布的信息公开（FOI）调查，向英格兰 73 家 NHS 信托提问：2026 年有没有把特定高危病人放在急诊科的走廊里。回答是有，41 家（56%）。而这批人正是 NHS England 指南写明「必须避免」走廊照护的对象——儿童、精神健康患者、有躯体或学习障碍的人。

儿童部分最具体：10 家信托（14%）承认放儿童进过走廊，其中一家确认最小 15 岁；另有 4 家说最小 16 岁，1 家说「都是 16 岁以上」，1 家 17 岁，还有 3 家无法确认。North West Anglia 信托说自 2026 年 1 月 1 日以来发生过 4 次。

规模数据来自 NHS 自己的统计：2026 年 10 月，平均每天 2,718 名患者在急诊走廊接受治疗；这个数字自 6 月公布以来在夏季大致持平，进入冬季通常会上升。皇家急诊医学院（RCEM）11 月初的调查则显示，1/5 的患者经历过走廊照护，其中就包括精神危机患者与儿童。

口径要写清三点。FOI 问的是「有没有发生过」，不是「发生多频繁」，所以 56% 是下限；调查覆盖急诊科，不含精神科病房与院前环节；上月卫生与社会保障大臣 Yvette Cooper 刚承诺「消除」儿童走廊照护，并要求 NHS England 制定紧急新标准——这组数据是承诺之后的现状。

看点这是执行缺口，不是知识缺口。指南已经写明「必须避免」，56% 的信托仍然发生过，说明约束不在「知不知道」，而在床位、人力与出院通道——只要精神科床位要等、社区支持住房没着落，急诊走廊就是唯一还开着的地方。对精神科患者，代价是双份的：没有隐私、没有可以固定风险的环境，而观察等级往往按躯体病人来设。要判断有没有改善，别只盯总数：把「高危人群是否被放在走廊」单列成一个可核查的指标、按信托公开，比一年一次的 FOI 更能形成压力。数据自身的局限也要说：41 家是「自报曾发生」，愿意承认的信托可能恰恰是记录更完整的那些，真实分布可能更偏。

29 名抑郁女性的交叉试验：500 毫克孕烯醇酮降低了杏仁核—额极连接，800 毫克反而没有

得克萨斯大学西南医学中心（UT Southwestern）2026 年 11 月 18 日发布，论文发表在《Journal of Clinical Psychiatry》。通讯作者 E. Sherwood Brown 是该校精神科教授、心理神经内分泌研究项目主任，合作方包括得州大学达拉斯分校的脑健康中心（Center for BrainHealth）。

设计是双盲、安慰剂对照的交叉试验：29 名轻中度重性抑郁的女性，先后接受三种各 7 天的条件——孕烯醇酮 500 毫克/日、800 毫克/日、安慰剂，口服；每个条件做血清检测与静息态功能磁共振。

影像结果：与安慰剂相比，500 毫克/日降低了杏仁核与左侧额极之间的功能连接。前者管情绪反应，后者参与认知控制，这条通路在抑郁里常被描述成「情绪盖过了控制」；800 毫克/日没有出现同样的变化。两种剂量都显著升高了别孕烯醇酮（allopregnanolone，一种下游神经甾体），耐受良好，没有报告严重不良事件。

口径必须写清：29 人入组，最后能进入影像分析的只有 11 人——三种条件都有可用 fMRI 数据的人。作者把下一步定为两件事：这些连接变化是否与症状或认知改善相关，以及在更大样本里重复。

看点最值得记的不是「某种激素改善了抑郁」，而是那条非单调的剂量曲线：500 毫克有变化、800 毫克没有。如果它站得住，现有「剂量越高、受体占用越多」的直觉在这条通路上就是错的，剂量选择会变成必须做参数扫描的事，而不是简单加量。但这条曲线目前架在 11 个人身上：交叉设计里每一次给药都会改变下一次的基线，携带效应与顺序效应都可能造出一个假的「中剂量最优」；而且研究没有把连接变化与症状挂钩，等于只给了机制的一半。要让它进入临床讨论，至少需要一批百人量级、平行分组、以症状为主要终点、并预先注册剂量比较的试验。

11/18 Chinese Academy of Sciences（EurekAlert） ↗

145 人的试验：连续三天无创深部电刺激 vmPFC，10 Hz 改善亚临床抑郁、一周后仍在，5 Hz 无效

《Science Translational Medicine》2026 年 11 月 18 日发表，中国科学院心理研究所涂毅恒团队与深圳大学等合作完成（据中国科学院总部新闻稿；AAAS 同日发布研究摘要）。

第一步是定位靶点。团队把功能磁共振、脑电与计算建模合起来，锁定一条连接腹内侧前额叶（vmPFC）与腹侧纹状体的奖赏回路，并发现 vmPFC 的 theta 与 alpha 频段振荡和「奖赏预测误差」相关——也就是预期与实际拿到的东西之间的差。

第二步是把参数做出来。他们用经颅时间干涉刺激（tTIS），通过头皮电极让两路电流在深部交汇，不用开刀就能作用到 vmPFC。76 名健康成人先验证安全与参数特异性：10 Hz 选择性增强奖赏学习、改变了额纹状体环路的耦合，5 Hz 没有同等效果。

第三步是临床试验：69 名有亚临床抑郁症状的人，随机、双盲、假刺激对照，连续 3 天、每天 3 次。治疗结束 1 天后，10 Hz 组的抑郁评分下降显著大于假刺激组；7 天随访时效果仍在。

口径：受试者是「亚临床」症状，不是确诊的抑郁障碍；两篇新闻稿都没有给出效应量、置信区间与脱落人数；作者明确说这不是闭环脑机接口，并称需要更大规模临床试验才能确立疗效。

看点这条的卖点不是「电刺激能改善情绪」，而是「不用开刀就能到 vmPFC」。深部脑刺激（DBS）对难治性抑郁有证据，但要手术；tTIS 用的是头皮电极和两路干涉电流，设备成本与风险都低一个数量级，如果 10 Hz 这个参数的特异性成立，它同时是疗效假设和工艺参数。三个问题决定它能走多远：特异性——5 Hz 无效是好信号，但需要独立重复；时间尺度——3 天 9 次、随访 1 周，慢性抑郁要的是数月；人群——亚临床样本的天花板效应低，真正难的是确诊人群与难治人群。对支付方，设备一旦便宜，问题会从「谁能做」变成「谁来评估、按什么标准收费」。

11/18 Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health (EurekAlert) ↗

138 studies, 35,296 students: intolerance of uncertainty has climbed 0.65 points a year since 1992, and three times faster since 2012

The paper is in JAMA Psychiatry; Columbia University's Mailman School of Public Health announced it on November 18, 2026. Jeroen M. Van Baar, a postdoctoral associate in epidemiology at Boston University's School of Public Health, is first author; Katherine M. Keyes, professor of epidemiology at Columbia Mailman, is senior author. Co-authors include R. Nicholas Carleton's lab at the University of Regina. The work was supported by NIMH grant 5T32MH013043, and the authors report no financial conflicts.

The method is a stitch-up of decades. Searching Web of Science for peer-reviewed studies of US and Canadian college students published between 2007 and 2025, the team screened nearly 300 full texts and kept 138 studies covering 35,296 people who had completed the 27-item or 12-item Intolerance of Uncertainty Scale, then regressed mean scores on year of data collection.

Average scores rose about 0.65 points a year from 1992 through 2023, with an inflection around 2012: the climb between 2012 and 2023 ran at roughly three times the 1992–2011 rate. As the authors put it, IU is neither a health outcome nor a root cause of disease but an intermediate cognitive process feeding anxiety — needing to know exactly what a class or a party will be like before walking in, and distress when that cannot be known.

Three caveats travel with the number. First, this is not one cohort followed for 31 years; it is cross-sections from different years and different samples, so a population-level shift cannot be read as "this year's students are more fragile". Second, IUS-27 and IUS-12 are pooled, which puts the comparability of scores across studies inside the finding rather than outside it. Third, the authors confront the reporting-artefact objection directly: IU has little currency outside psychology, so a rise is less likely to be a vocabulary effect — an argument worth taking seriously, but still an argument.

The practical edge is that interventions targeting IU already work. If the trend is real, the target is more specific and more measurable than "anxiety" as a whole.

Why it mattersWhat shifts here is not "young people are more anxious" but the burden of proof in the argument over why. A population-level rise has two readings — real change, or a lower threshold for reporting — and the study picks a construct with almost no public currency precisely to make the second harder to defend. Grade the evidence in layers. Breadth is real: 138 studies, 35,000 people, 31 years. Inference is weaker: cross-sectional data cannot say whether the same people changed, and cannot exclude the smartphones, academic competition and economic insecurity that arrived alongside. The authors themselves only call these factors intertwined. Two results would settle it: a longitudinal cohort showing IU scores predicting later anxiety-disorder onset, and a randomised trial showing an IU-targeted intervention lowers that onset.
Another angle Turn 0.65 points a year into scale: extrapolated linearly, 31 years comes to roughly 20 points, and IUS-27 is 27 items scored 1 to 5. The gap between two generations on the same form is no longer "a question or two answered differently".

The design is as plain as it gets: interviews with 21 first-time fathers in Sweden whose children were between 10 and 24 months old, the window in which milk gives way to family food and pickiness starts to show.

One tension runs through the transcripts. Why it mattersThis will usually be filed under "fathers are not involved enough", but what it actually documents is a mismatch in service design: contacts with child health services thin out with age while uncertainty about feeding peaks between 10 and 24 months. 29 women, one crossover trial: 500 mg of pregnenolone lowered amygdala–frontal pole coupling, and 800 mg did not

UT Southwestern Medical Center announced the findings on November 18, 2026; the paper is in the Journal of Clinical Psychiatry. E. Sherwood Brown, professor of psychiatry and director of the psychoneuroendocrine research programme, is corresponding author, with colleagues from the Center for BrainHealth at UT Dallas.

The design was a double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover: 29 women with mild to moderate major depressive disorder each received three seven-day conditions — pregnenolone at 500 mg a day, at 800 mg a day, and placebo, taken orally — with serum sampling and resting-state fMRI around each.

On imaging, 500 mg a day reduced functional connectivity between the amygdala and the left frontal pole compared with placebo. The amygdala drives emotional reactivity, the frontal pole participates in cognitive control, and the pathway is often described in depression as emotion overriding control. The 800 mg dose produced no comparable change. Both doses significantly raised allopregnanolone, a downstream neurosteroid, and both were tolerated with no serious adverse events reported.

The caveats are not small: 29 women enrolled, and the imaging analysis rested on 11 who had usable data from all three conditions. The authors name the next steps themselves — whether these connectivity changes track symptom or cognitive improvement, and replication in a larger sample.

Why it mattersThe finding worth keeping is not "a hormone helped depression" but the shape of the dose curve: 500 mg moved the circuit, 800 mg did not. If that holds, the intuition that more drug means more receptor occupancy is wrong on this pathway, and dose selection becomes a parameter sweep rather than a step up. But the curve currently rests on eleven people. In a crossover, each dose changes the baseline for the next, so carry-over and order effects can manufacture a spurious mid-dose optimum; and the study never links connectivity to symptoms, which is half a mechanism. Getting this into clinical argument needs a parallel-group trial in the low hundreds, with symptoms as the primary endpoint and the dose comparison pre-registered.

11/18 Chinese Academy of Sciences (EurekAlert) ↗

145 people, three days of non-invasive deep stimulation: 10 Hz eased subclinical depression a week later, 5 Hz did not

The study appeared in Science Translational Medicine on November 18, 2026, led by Tu Yiheng's group at the Institute of Psychology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, with Shenzhen University and other institutions, according to the academy's release; AAAS published a summary the same day.

Step one was finding the target. Combining fMRI, EEG and computational modelling, the team identified a reward circuit linking the ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC) to the ventral striatum, and found that theta- and alpha-band oscillations in the vmPFC tracked reward prediction error — the gap between what was expected and what arrived.

Step two was building the parameters. Their protocol uses transcranial temporal interference stimulation (tTIS), in which two currents delivered through scalp electrodes intersect deep in the brain, reaching the vmPFC without surgery. In 76 healthy adults it proved safe and frequency-specific: 10 Hz selectively enhanced reward learning and altered coupling in the frontostriatal circuit, while 5 Hz produced no comparable benefit.

Step three was the trial: 69 people with subclinical depressive symptoms, randomised, double-blind and sham-controlled, three sessions a day for three days. One day after treatment the 10 Hz group's depression scores had fallen significantly further than sham, and the difference was still there at seven days.

The qualifiers: participants had subclinical symptoms, not diagnosed depressive disorder. Neither release reports effect sizes, confidence intervals or dropouts. The authors are explicit that this is not a closed-loop brain-computer interface, and that larger clinical trials are needed before efficacy can be claimed.

Why it mattersThe selling point is not that electricity lifts mood; it is that the vmPFC can be reached without opening a skull. Deep brain stimulation has evidence in treatment-resistant depression but requires surgery, while tTIS runs on scalp electrodes and interfered currents, an order of magnitude cheaper and safer. If the 10 Hz specificity holds, that frequency is simultaneously the therapeutic hypothesis and the process patent. Three questions decide how far it travels. Specificity: 5 Hz failing is a good sign, but it needs independent replication. Duration: nine sessions over three days with one week of follow-up, against a chronic condition measured in months. Population: subclinical samples have a low ceiling, and the hard cases are diagnosed and treatment-resistant patients. For payers, once the hardware is cheap the question stops being who can offer it and becomes who assesses it, and against which tariff.
