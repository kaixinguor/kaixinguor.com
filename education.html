河南把公办学校食堂收回自营：1.2 万所学校统一采购，食材成本降 31.5%

《中国教育报》11 月 16 日报道，河南持续推进校园餐专项整治，把公办学校食堂收回外包、转为学校自主经营，切断外包模式容易滋生的利益链条。鹤壁市高中实行食堂零利润运营，明确食材成本占比不低于 65%、人工成本控制在 30% 以内，结余资金用于食堂设备维护升级；漯河市郾城中学的中餐标准是 5 元至 6 元，一份米饭套餐两荤两素，主食可以再添，每学期还有 4 至 5 次全校师生免费吃的爱心餐。

采购环节被单独拆出来管。鹤壁全市公办学校接入「阳光餐饮」智慧监管平台，供应商线上竞价，食材直接配送入校，绕开中间承包商。据当地统计，改革之后学校食材原料成本下降 31.5%，学生用餐满意度从 75.1% 提升到 98.3%。市级监管平台普遍搭载 AI 预警识别功能，后厨监控全覆盖，对未佩戴口罩、食材混放等 20 多项不规范操作自动识别预警。

省级层面的三个数字是：河南 18 个地市均已建成市级校园餐监管平台，1.2 万余所学校通过平台统一采购，1.6 万多所学校纳入餐费统一监管。监督一侧，全省落实校领导陪餐、家长陪餐制度，家长膳食委员会成员达到 16.1 万人，上半年各类监督检查累计开展 5.47 万次。

制度上，河南建立问题、责任、整改 3 类台账，按「发现问题—限期整改—复核销号—回头看」闭环管理，并出台校园餐标准指引，紧盯采购、经费、运营各环节。漯河还明确食堂不得外购饺子馅等半成品，须由后厨现场加工，以消除食品安全隐患。

看点收回外包改变的不只是经营权，还有责任分配：学校从收租方变成运营方，校长成了食品安全的第一责任人；而食材成本 65%、人工成本 30% 这两条线一旦写进考核，食堂就没有靠餐费腾挪的空间。1.2 万所学校统一采购把议价权集中到市级平台，价格下来了，风险也集中了——集采或平台环节出一次问题，波及的是全市而不是一校。16.1 万家长陪餐、5.47 万次检查，摊到 1.6 万多所学校，每校半年约三次多，这套监督的相当一部分成本由家长的无酬劳动承担。
另一个视角餐费统一监管，等于把一笔长期沉淀在承包商和学校账上的现金流，转到平台与专户结算，对县级财政是新增的现金流管理与人手。鹤壁用「零利润＋结余维护」替代折旧计提，设备更新的钱实际上还是从 30% 的人工成本里挤，这笔账要到两三年后设备换代时才会显出来。

HSK 留学中国预科联盟成立：36 家中外院校发起，国外学中文、国内补数理化各半年

11 月 15 日，2026 世界中文大会期间，HSK 留学中国预科联盟在北京成立。这是首个面向外国中文学习者、采用「国外＋国内」两段式开展预科教育的中外合作机制，指导单位包括教育部国际合作与交流司、教育部中外语言交流合作中心、国家留学基金管理委员会、教育部留学服务中心和中国国际中文教育基金会。

首批发起方为 36 家中外大学与教育机构，另有国家开放大学、外语教学与研究出版社等 4 家支持单位。外方包括巴西里约热内卢大学、德国汉园中文学习测试中心、吉尔吉斯国立民族大学、泰国玫瑰园中学；中方包括北京大学、复旦大学、上海交通大学等。

课程分成两段：国外阶段以中文和中国国情教育为主，国内阶段以中文＋数学、物理、化学等课程为主。完成国外阶段学习的学生进入国内合作院校继续培养半年，达标后进入本科学习。联盟按「政府指导、协商合作、平等自愿、市场运作」的原则运行，由「HSK 留学中国与就业展」主办方汉考国际负责运营。

联盟要对接的需求端规模是：HSK 已在 169 个国家设立 1500 余个考点，考点覆盖国家数量居全球语言考试首位，累计服务考生超过 900 万人次。

看点这套设计把语言门槛和学科门槛拆开了：语言在生源国解决，成本由当地机构与合作方承担；学科与预科在国内解决，半年时间、住宿与师资由合作高校承担。真正决定质量的是「达标」两个字——标准谁定、由谁考核、不达标的学生往哪里去，报道里都没有写。由汉考国际运营，意味着联盟靠考试与展会的收入运转、不靠财政拨款，这会让它更愿意扩规模，而不是压标准。

韩国税务师考试第一名承认用 AI 作弊：160 题只错 1 题，青瓦台要求高考严防

新华社 11 月 15 日消息，在韩国注册税务师考试中以超高分数获得第一名的考生当天承认，自己在考试中使用人工智能工具作弊，并向首尔地方警察厅自首。这名男性考生 30 多岁，姓名没有对外公布。

在今年举行的这场考试中，他在第一轮 160 道题里只错 1 题，接近满分，第二轮各科目也都获得高分，异常优异的成绩引发质疑。他通过视频平台承认，自己利用微型摄像头和 AI 工具作弊，并在注册会计师考试中使用了同样手段。按照韩国相关法律，被认定作弊者的成绩将被取消，5 年内不得再次参加该考试。

事件曝光当晚，韩国总统府青瓦台召开紧急会议商讨防范措施。青瓦台政策室长河骏坰在会上要求教育部采取措施，在定于 11 月 19 日举行的大学修学能力考试（即韩国高考）中杜绝这类行为；国务总理韩圣淑定于 16 日召集教育部、雇佣劳动部、行政安全部等相关政府部门官员进一步讨论应对措施。

今年以来，韩国已查处多起考生利用 AI 工具及设备作弊的事件，涉案场景覆盖各类等级考试。韩国教育部今年 7 月把 AI 眼镜列入高考禁带物品清单，本月 9 日发布的「考生注意事项指南」中，明确禁止考生携带包括 AI 眼镜在内的任何电子设备进入考场。

看点防作弊的成本正在从「监考人力」转向「设备清单＋入场检查」：AI 眼镜可以列清单，微型摄像头不行。更麻烦的是发现机制——这次是靠「分数高得不像话」事后暴露，而不是入场时被拦住。当异常高分成为唯一可靠的报警器，考试机构实际上把成本推给了事后调查与司法程序；而 5 年禁考的处罚，对一个能拿到执业资格的人来说，收益与风险的算法未必划算。

全国商业智能服务行业产教融合共同体成立：用友、中央财大、山东商职大牵头，首批 378 家单位

据山东商业职业技术大学 11 月 15 日发布的消息，11 月 14 日，全国商业智能服务行业产教融合共同体成立大会在济南举行，来自全国的 300 余位嘉宾参加。教育部职业教育发展中心战略发展处副处长苏敏、山东省教育厅职业教育处处长陈志浩、山东省商业集团有限公司党委副书记、董事张洪东受邀出席；共同体由中国商业联合会指导。

牵头方是三家：用友网络科技股份有限公司、中央财经大学、山东商业职业技术大学；新道科技股份有限公司与山东商业职业技术大学担任秘书长单位。首批成员汇聚全国高等学校、职业学校、科研机构、行业组织和产业链上下游企业共 378 家。

成立会上为 38 家常务副理事长单位、58 家副理事长单位现场授牌，并发布共同体章程与第一届理事会名单、建设方案和重点任务。主题报告与圆桌对话围绕共同体建设改革、教学关键要素联动改革、高技能人才集群培养，以及「科技兴商」背景下的五要素联动改革展开。

共同体对外给出的落点是一条传导机制：「产业场景—岗位任务—能力标准—教学要素」。业务方向面向商贸流通、现代物流、供应链管理、财税金融、商务咨询和企业经营管理，依托人工智能推动数据、算法、智能体、智能装备与业务流程融合。

看点这类共同体的实质，是把「岗位任务」的定价权交给企业：由用友这样的厂商定义软件流程与岗位能力，学校据此改课程、买实训平台，教学要素跟着产品走。378 家单位里只有 96 家拿到常务副理事长和副理事长席位，话语权是集中的。对职业本科来说，这是把实训软件采购与课程标准捆在一起的机会，代价是可能被单一厂商的技术路线锁定——换一套 ERP，课程与教材要跟着重写。

昆山为 2.7 万名教师统一开通 AI 助手账号：不用申请，用登记手机号激活

据芥末堆 11 月 15 日报道，昆山市教育局宣布，已为全市 2.7 万名教师统一开通 WorkBuddy 教育公益版专属账号。账号面向全市在职教师完成统一预开通，无需额外提交申请，教师使用本人在教育系统登记的手机号码即可登录激活全部权益。

昆山市教育局给出的用法，是备课、写教案、做课件、出分层作业、整理教研材料这类高频、耗时但「有章可循」的任务：教师说明教学目标、班级情况与想要的初稿，AI 产出后可以在原稿上迭代，不必从零撰写提示词。产品方称，教育公益版联合北京大学、北京师范大学等高校内置了 100 多名教育专家角色，覆盖教学设计、公开课打磨、家校沟通、学生心理等场景。

昆山此前已整合多款教育大模型，做了「智教星」「智护星」「AI 学伴」等垂直教育智能体，形成「一云多端」的「AI＋教育」生态，并围绕教师数字素养提升、智能体开发应用、人机协同教学三条主线推进。

同类部署可以对照：8 月 26 日，山东莱州面向全市 6000 多名中小学教师配备该 AI 助教；10 月 19 日教育公益版上线后，深圳福田是首个整体落地的城区，覆盖全区 110 多所学校、近 1.3 万名教师；同月无锡梁溪区为全区 8000 多名教职工配备账号，覆盖率达到 100%，一个月内汇集 9000 件教师自主开发的 AI 教学应用。

看点统一预开通绕开了「教师自己申请」这道采纳门槛，把成本从每个教师的学习时间，转成教育局的一次性部署与后续培训。2.7 万个账号本身几乎不产生边际支出，真正的开支在教研组织、案例筛选和「用了没有」的考核上——梁溪一个月攒出 9000 件应用，说明供给不缺，缺的是把应用筛成课程的那道工序。报道里没有出现学生数据与教师使用记录的归属约定，这恰恰是这类区县统采最该先写清楚的一条。

「爱中文」学习社区发布全球伙伴计划：500 多家机构入驻，269 项成果评出 32 个案例

11 月 15 日，2026 世界中文大会期间，「爱中文」学习社区全球伙伴大会在北京召开，会上发布「爱中文」学习社区全球伙伴计划与国际中文教育人工智能创新应用典型案例。教育部中外语言交流合作中心主任郁云峰表示，社区将全面融入人工智能，实现供需精准适配、打造全程闭环链条。

社区以数字基座为基础、以人工智能为驱动，为全球师生和机构提供个性化智能教学服务，目前已有 500 多家中外机构入驻。此次发布的全球伙伴计划，面向全球高校、孔子学院（课堂）、教育文化机构、国际组织及行业协会、科技企业、出版机构等开放。

案例一侧的数字是：教育部中外语言交流合作中心此前发起的首届国际中文教育人工智能创新应用案例征集，共收到全球 20 个国家、129 家单位报送的 269 项成果，经评审最终选出 32 个典型案例。

商业一侧，会上发布了三款合作产品：章鱼时代新版 Chinesia APP、荣耀 C-Lingo 中文智慧学习硬件、智慧宫智慧中文 APP（阿语 AI 版），并举行 2026 中文联盟金牌项目颁奖仪式。会议由语合中心主办，五洲汉风网络科技（北京）有限公司、深圳市章鱼时代科技发展有限公司承办；老挝国立大学校长达拉翁·辛塔湾、摩洛哥职业教育与就业发展署总干事路布纳·特里查出席并致辞。

看点语合中心把平台的具体运营交给市场主体承办（五洲汉风、章鱼时代），自己保留标准、案例评选与「金牌项目」这类认证，是典型的「公共标准＋商业运营」分工。500 多家机构入驻只是供给侧规模，伙伴计划真正的条款是分成比例与数据归属，而这两条报道没有披露。269 项成果只评出 32 个案例，命中率约 12%，说明评审仍以示范为主、还不是采购目录——对想进校园的企业，示范名单不等于订单。

Henan takes public school canteens back in-house: 12,000 schools on one buying platform, ingredient costs down 31.5%

Henan is pushing its school-meal clean-up further by taking canteens in public schools back from outside contractors and running them in-house, China Education Daily reported on November 16. Hebi Senior High School operates its canteen at zero profit, with ingredients required to make up at least 65% of the meal price and labour held under 30%, and the surplus spent on kitchen equipment. At Yancheng Middle School in Luohe, a 5 to 6 yuan lunch buys two meat and two vegetable dishes with free refills, and four or five times a term pupils and staff eat for nothing.

Procurement has been separated out and tightened. Every public school in Hebi is connected to a "sunshine catering" platform where suppliers bid online and food is delivered straight to the school gate, cutting out the middleman. The city says ingredient costs have fallen 31.5% and pupil satisfaction has risen from 75.1% to 98.3%. City platforms also carry AI monitoring: kitchen cameras are everywhere, and the software flags more than 20 kinds of breach, from an uncovered head to raw and cooked food stored together.

Province-wide the figures are these: all 18 prefecture-level cities have built a school-meal platform; more than 12,000 schools buy through it; more than 16,000 have their meal fees under unified supervision. On the oversight side, principals and parents now eat with pupils as a matter of course, parent meal committees have 161,000 members, and 54,700 inspections were carried out in the first half of 2026.

Henan keeps three ledgers - problems, responsibilities, rectification - and works a closed loop from detection to deadline, sign-off and look-back, backed by a standards guideline covering procurement, money and operations. Luohe has banned canteens from buying ready-made dumpling filling and requires it to be made on site.

Why it mattersTaking canteens back changes more than who holds the lease: the school moves from landlord to operator, and the head teacher becomes the first person accountable for food safety. Once ingredients at 65% and labour at 30% are written into the assessment, there is no slack left to move money around. Centralised buying for 12,000 schools concentrates bargaining power in the city platform, which pushes prices down and concentrates risk at the same time: one failure in procurement or on the platform hits a whole city, not one school. The 161,000 parents on meal committees and 54,700 inspections work out at roughly three or four checks per school per half-year, and a good part of that oversight is unpaid.
Another angle Unified supervision of meal fees moves a large cash flow that used to sit with contractors and school accounts into platform and earmarked-account settlement, which is new cash-management work and new headcount for county finance. Hebi has replaced depreciation with a zero-profit model that funds equipment out of the surplus, so replacement money still comes out of the 30% labour line; that bill arrives when the kitchens are refitted, not this year.

11/16 China Education Daily ↗

HSK pre-university alliance launches with 36 institutions, splitting preparation between home country and China

The HSK Pre-University Alliance for Study in China was launched in Beijing on November 15 during the 2026 World Chinese Language Conference. It is the first Chinese-foreign mechanism to prepare foreign learners for a Chinese degree in two stages, one abroad and one in China, and it is guided by five bodies: the education ministry's department of international cooperation and exchanges, the Center for Language Education and Cooperation, the China Scholarship Council, the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange and the China International Chinese Education Foundation.

Thirty-six Chinese and foreign universities and education bodies are founding members, with four supporting institutions including the Open University of China and the Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press. The foreign members include Rio de Janeiro State University in Brazil, the Hanyuan Chinese language testing centre in Germany, Kyrgyz National University and Rose Garden School in Thailand; Chinese members include Peking University, Fudan University and Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

The curriculum is split in two. Abroad, the focus is Chinese and knowledge about China; in China, it is Chinese plus mathematics, physics and chemistry. Students who finish the overseas stage spend another half year at a partner university in China and enter an undergraduate programme once they meet the standard. The alliance runs on the principle of "government guidance, consultation, voluntary participation and market operation", and is operated by Hanyu Kaoshi International, the organiser of the HSK Study in China and Careers fairs.

The demand it is built for: HSK now has more than 1,500 test centres in 169 countries, the widest country coverage of any language test, and has served more than nine million candidates in total.

Why it mattersThe design separates two barriers that used to be cleared together. Language is handled in the student's own country, at the cost of local partners; subjects and preparation are handled in China, where the host university provides the half year, the dormitory and the teachers. Quality turns on the word "standard": who sets it, who tests it, and where a student goes if they miss it are all absent from the announcement. Putting a test operator in charge means the alliance lives on exam and fair revenue rather than a budget line, which gives it a reason to grow the intake rather than hold the line.

Top scorer in Korea's tax accountant exam admits AI cheating as the Blue House orders a crackdown before the college test

The candidate who came first in South Korea's registered tax accountant examination admitted on November 15 that he had used artificial intelligence to cheat, and turned himself in to the Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency, Xinhua reported. The man is in his thirties and has not been named.

In this year's exam he got 159 of 160 questions right in the first round, close to a perfect score, and posted high marks in every subject of the second round, a result so far above the field that it drew suspicion. In a video posted online he said he had used a miniature camera together with AI tools, and had done the same in the certified public accountant exam. Under Korean law a candidate found to have cheated loses the result and is barred from the exam for five years.

The presidential office convened an emergency meeting that evening. Policy chief Ha Joon-kyung told the education ministry to make sure nothing of the kind happens in the national college scholastic ability test on November 19, and Prime Minister Han Seong-sook called a meeting of the education, employment and labour, and interior and safety ministries for November 16.

Korea has caught a series of AI-assisted cheating cases this year across different levels of examination. The education ministry put AI glasses on the list of items banned from the college test in July, and its candidate guidance issued on November 9 explicitly forbids any electronic device, AI glasses included, in the exam room.

Why it mattersThe cost of prevention is shifting from invigilation to the banned-items list and the door check: AI glasses can be listed, a miniature camera cannot. The harder problem is detection. This case surfaced because the score looked impossible, not because anyone stopped him at the door. Once an outlier score is the only reliable alarm, exam boards push the cost onto after-the-fact investigation and prosecution; and a five-year ban may not outweigh the payoff for someone who ends up with a professional licence.

Business-intelligence industry-education consortium launches in Jinan with 378 members and UFIDA as lead

The national industry-education consortium for business-intelligence services held its founding meeting in Jinan on November 14, with more than 300 guests from across the country, according to a report published by Shandong Vocational University of Commerce on November 15. Su Min, deputy director of the strategy division at the education ministry's vocational education development centre, Chen Zhihao, head of vocational education at the Shandong education department, and Zhang Hongdong, deputy party secretary and director of Shandong Commercial Group, attended; the China General Chamber of Commerce guides the consortium.

Three bodies lead it: UFIDA (Yonyou Network Technology), the Central University of Finance and Economics, and Shandong Vocational University of Commerce. Seentao Technology and Shandong Vocational University of Commerce serve as secretariat. The first cohort brings together 378 members - universities, vocational schools, research institutes, industry bodies and companies along the supply chain.

The meeting awarded plaques to 38 standing vice-chair units and 58 vice-chair units, and released the charter, the first council list, the construction plan and key tasks. Sessions covered consortium reform, the linking of key teaching elements, cluster training for high-skill talent, and the "five elements" reform under the banner of technology-driven commerce.

The mechanism the consortium promises runs from industry scenario to job task, competence standard and teaching element. Its sectors are wholesale and retail, modern logistics, supply chain management, tax and finance, business consulting and corporate management, with AI used to tie data, algorithms, agents and smart equipment into business processes.

Why it mattersThe substance of a consortium like this is that companies set the price of a job task: a vendor such as UFIDA defines the software workflow and the competences, schools rewrite curricula and buy training platforms to match, and teaching follows the product. Only 96 of the 378 members hold a standing or ordinary vice-chair seat, so the say is concentrated. For a vocational undergraduate university this is a chance to tie platform purchases to curriculum standards; the price is lock-in to one vendor's technical road map, since swapping the ERP means rewriting the courses and the textbooks.

Kunshan switches on an AI assistant account for all 27,000 teachers, no application needed

Kunshan's education bureau has opened a WorkBuddy education edition account for all 27,000 teachers in the city, Jiemodui reported on November 15. The accounts were pre-activated for every serving teacher, with no application to file; staff log in with the mobile number already registered in the education system and get the full set of features.

The bureau frames the use case around frequent, time-consuming but rule-bound work: preparing lessons, writing plans, building slides, setting tiered homework and sorting teaching-research material. A teacher states the objective, the class and the draft wanted, and the tool produces one to be revised, so nobody has to write prompts from scratch. The product side says the education edition, built with Peking University and Beijing Normal University among others, ships more than 100 expert roles covering lesson design, open-class polishing, parent communication and pupil wellbeing.

Kunshan had already assembled several education models into agents - "Zhijiao Xing", "Zhihu Xing" and an AI study companion - under a "one cloud, many devices" stack, and is working along three lines: teacher digital literacy, agent development, and human-machine co-teaching.

Comparable rollouts: Laizhou in Shandong gave the assistant to more than 6,000 primary and secondary teachers on August 26; after the education edition launched on October 19, Shenzhen's Futian district became the first to deploy it district-wide, across more than 110 schools and nearly 13,000 teachers; the same month Liangxi in Wuxi equipped more than 8,000 staff for 100% coverage and collected 9,000 teacher-built AI teaching applications within a month.

Why it mattersPre-activating accounts removes the adoption step where teachers decide for themselves: the cost moves from each teacher's learning time to a one-off deployment and the training that follows. The 27,000 accounts themselves cost almost nothing at the margin; the real spending is on teaching-research organisation, filtering what teachers build, and checking whether anyone uses it. Liangxi's 9,000 applications in a month suggest supply is not the constraint - turning applications into courses is. What the report never mentions is who owns pupil data and teacher usage logs, which is the clause a district-wide purchase should settle first.

Ai Zhongwen community opens a global partner programme: 500-plus institutions aboard, 32 cases chosen from 269

The Ai Zhongwen learning community held a global partners meeting in Beijing on November 15 during the 2026 World Chinese Language Conference, releasing a partner programme and a set of model cases in AI for international Chinese teaching. Yu Yunfeng, director of the education ministry's Center for Language Education and Cooperation, said the community will embed AI throughout, matching supply to demand and closing the loop from learning to assessment.

Built on a digital base and driven by AI, the community serves learners, teachers and institutions worldwide; more than 500 Chinese and foreign institutions have joined. The partner programme is open to universities, Confucius Institutes and classrooms, education and cultural bodies, international organisations and industry associations, technology companies and publishers.

On the case side, a first call for AI applications in international Chinese education drew 269 submissions from 129 organisations in 20 countries, and 32 were selected as model cases.

On the commercial side, three partner products were released: a new version of Zhangyu Times' Chinesia app, Honor's C-Lingo Chinese learning hardware, and Zhihuigong's Arabic-language AI app. The meeting also handed out the 2026 Chinese Language Alliance gold project awards. The Center for Language Education and Cooperation hosted it, with Wuzhou Hanfeng Network Technology and Shenzhen Zhangyu Times as organisers; the president of the National University of Laos and the director-general of Morocco's vocational training agency spoke.

Why it mattersThe language center keeps the standard, the case selection and the gold-project badge while handing day-to-day operation to companies, a familiar split between public standards and commercial delivery. Five hundred institutions is a supply-side headcount; the clauses that matter in a partner programme are revenue share and data ownership, and neither is disclosed. Thirty-two cases out of 269 is a hit rate of about 12%, which says the list is still a showcase rather than a procurement catalogue - for a company hoping to get into classrooms, a model case is not an order.
