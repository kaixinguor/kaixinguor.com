11/10 11:37 The Paper ↗

NDRC: school-age numbers have peaked for kindergartens and compulsory schooling, and senior-high places must follow the children

At the fourth briefing in its "stride into the 15th Five-Year Plan" series on November 10, China's National Development and Reform Commission focused on livelihoods. Huang Weiru, a deputy director in its social development department, said enrolment has already peaked for kindergartens, primary schools and junior-high schools, while senior-high and higher-education numbers keep rising, so the allocation of education resources has to change. Over the 14th Five-Year Plan period, the three stages together lost nearly 17 million students.

For the capacity that frees up, the NDRC wants coordination: pooling school buildings across urban and rural areas and across school stages inside a county, moving toward smaller classes, and converting vacated space into rooms for labour education and the arts. In higher education, Double First-Class universities added 2.4 million undergraduate places in 2026, mostly in fields of strategic need; central investment support will be tied to that expansion, and localities able to do so are encouraged to build new research universities and high-level vocational undergraduate institutions. Between 2021 and 2025 the urban-to-rural enrolment ratio in basic education rose from about 8:2 to about 9:1.

Why it mattersThe real shift is the basis for allocating resources: from household registration to the resident school-age population. Tying central investment to Double First-Class expansion trades fiscal leverage for elite undergraduate places, while senior-high expansion directly changes how junior-high graduates are streamed. For localities, money and teaching posts have to follow the places; for families, what eases is the fear of having no senior-high seat at all, not the competition for the best ones. The hard part is the third stage: whether vacated primary-school buildings can become senior-high classrooms depends on how posts and funding move between stages.

11/11 China Education Daily ↗

Six universities renew support for Xiangtan University: 64 joint doctorates, 46 national social-science projects

On the afternoon of November 10, Xiangtan University held a meeting on its Double First-Class construction and launched a new round of the "6+1" joint programme on Marxist theory. Peking, Tsinghua, Renmin, Wuhan, Northeast Normal and Fudan universities signed with Xiangtan to renew cooperation, with Wuhan University again leading.

The new round focuses on developing senior talent, training top-notch students, improving ideological and political courses, building research platforms and sharing academic resources. Since the Ministry of Education organised five-university support in 2019 and the 6+1 agreement was renewed in 2023, the discipline has jointly trained 64 doctoral students, won 46 National Social Science Fund projects, and twice taken second prize in the national teaching achievement awards.

Why it mattersEvaluation of pairing support is moving from whether an agreement was signed to whether discipline metrics moved: 64 doctorates, 46 national projects and two teaching awards are auditable. The hidden cost sits with teachers at the lead universities, whose staff and teaching hours must be carved out of their own programmes. Whether this works will show in the next agreement: does it name the people seconded and include an exit mechanism?

11/11 China Education Daily ↗

Xinyang turns the Long March departure sites into living classrooms: 4,643 study visits in Luoshan this half-year

China Education Daily reported on November 11 that Xinyang in Henan, centred on Luoshan and Xinxian counties, has turned the departure sites of the Red 25th Army's Long March into living classrooms for ideological and political education. A ginkgo tree more than 800 years old at Hejiachong marks where the unit assembled in November 1934.

The numbers come in two tiers: Luoshan county organised 4,643 revolutionary-themed study visits in the first half of 2026 and links more than 20 sites along a scenic road, serving about 550,000 student visits a year citywide; Xinxian has trained more than 700 pupils as volunteer guides with local museums, hosting nearly 5,000 guided tours a year. The material is also folded into ordinary subjects: a junior-high maths class in Tiepu township uses the army's march distances and grain allocation as word problems, and primary science lessons build on the practical problems the marchers faced.

Why it mattersThe cost structure of this kind of teaching is usually left out: per-student transport, insurance and lost class time decide how many can be reached. The gap between 4,643 visits and 550,000 a year shows the living classroom depends heavily on city-level coordination and packaged routes; what lasts is the part folded into ordinary lessons, because it consumes no extra hours or budget. To replicate it, cost a visit per student first, then talk about coverage.

Vocational & Careers

职教与就业
11/11 Workers' Daily ↗

National workers' skills finals open in Zhuzhou on November 17: 570 competitors, AI trainer among the trades

The press conference for the finals of the ninth National Workers' Vocational Skills Competition was held in Changsha on November 10. The finals run from November 17 to 19 in Zhuzhou, Hunan, jointly staged by the All-China Federation of Trade Unions, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, and the science, industry, public security and Hunan provincial authorities.

Six trades are contested: CNC machine tool set-up and repair, intelligent connected-vehicle commissioning, AI trainer, electronic equipment assembly, industrial wastewater treatment, and app-based delivery. Thirty-two teams and 570 competitors advanced, with an average age of 33; theory counts for 30% and hands-on work for 70%. For the first time the contest ran a national online training phase, building question banks of 30,000 items and 300 subject courses across the six trades, free to all workers. Competitors ranked one to 20 in each trade move up a vocational skill grade, and the top three, where eligible, are recommended for the National May 1 Labour Medal.

Why it mattersThe trade list is an early disclosure of skill demand: AI trainers and connected-vehicle technicians entering a national contest shows the shortage has moved from the shop floor to data and algorithms. What changes supply is the back end: a top-20 finish converts directly into a higher skill grade and a bargaining chip on pay, a stronger draw than a medal. For colleges, the free question banks and 300 courses amount to a teaching standard they can align to.

Technology & Product

11/10 BLCU ↗

BLCU launches "Hanqiao": an AI agent for international Chinese teaching, built on 20 TB of material

On the afternoon of November 9, Beijing Language and Culture University held the launch of "Hanqiao", an AI agent for international Chinese language education, moving the project from development into pilot use and promotion. Officials attended from the Ministry of Education's language application and international cooperation departments, the Beijing municipal education commission and economy and IT bureau, and China Mobile Beijing.

Hanqiao draws on BLCU's multimodal language resources covering 189 countries and regions and more than 20 terabytes of material, combined with China Mobile's computing and network capacity. It covers teaching, learning, assessment and administration, from lesson preparation and resource generation to learner profiling, teaching evaluation and individual growth, on a principle that raw data never leaves campus and cloud services stay fully controlled. The event appointed the first cohort of Hanqiao "growth experience officers" - Confucius Institute directors, overseas campus heads and international students. BLCU and China Mobile Beijing signed a strategic cooperation agreement on September 19; more than 500 people attended the launch.

Why it mattersFor a domain-specific agent the test is not model parameters but whether pedagogy can be engineered: only by turning six decades of lesson plans, corpora and marking standards into a callable knowledge base does teaching find the learner. The risk sits in the same place - value-laden content must be reviewed and signed off by teachers, or scaling multiplies errors too; rights and licensing over 20 terabytes of material is the real cost line.
