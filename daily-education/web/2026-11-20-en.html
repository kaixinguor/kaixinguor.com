Policy & Regulation

政策与监管
11/19 14:26 Ministry of Education ↗

China expects 12.99 million graduates in 2027 as five central bodies set out a five-part jobs plan

On November 19 the secretariat of the Central Leading Group on Education Work, the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission held a joint conference in Beijing on employment and entrepreneurship for the 2027 graduating class. The projected size is 12.99 million. Huai Jinpeng, the education minister, and Wu Xiuzhang, a vice-minister of human resources, spoke, along with the industry ministry's chief engineer Zhong Zhihong and a senior official from the state-asset commission; vice-minister Xiong Sihao chaired.

For comparison, the 2026 cohort was projected at 12.70 million, up 480,000 year on year (China Education Television, November 2025). That puts the 2027 class 290,000 higher, a gain of about 2.3% (derived). The meeting defined its goal as keeping overall employment for the cohort "stable".

The five priorities are specific. On policy, use existing measures, back incremental ones and adjust recruitment calendars. On job creation, lift the carrying capacity of key regions, open space in advanced manufacturing and modern services, keep state-owned enterprises hiring, draw in small firms and support startups. On services, put public employment services on campus, deepen campus-local matching, push AI-enabled job matching and tilt support to universities with weak employment resources. On the safety net, pair disadvantaged graduates with helpers and keep serving those still unemployed after graduation. On industry, link enrolment, training and employment in a single review and expand company-built "micro-majors".

Reports came from Jiangsu's education leading group secretariat, the Tianjin human resources bureau, the Yantai city government, China Information and Communication Technologies Group, JD.com, Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology and Beijing City University. The meeting ran online and offline, with provincial counterparts joining remotely.

Why it matters12.99 million is a headcount, not a number of jobs. Of the five priorities the education ministry controls two: the joint review that feeds employment outcomes back into programme and enrolment decisions, and micro-majors that turn job skills into credit modules. Both run through academic affairs, not the careers office. The cohort is 290,000 larger, about 2.3%, so total pressure is roughly unchanged; the structural pressure is not. Putting "AI-enabled employment" under services says the policy leans on matching, and matching removes friction rather than creating vacancies.
Another angle"Back incremental policies" heads the list with no number attached. What can happen immediately is a shifted recruitment calendar, which stretches competition across the year: students pay in time, the budget does not move. And if universities are still judged on first-job placement rates, they will optimize for signatures rather than matches, and micro-majors will become one more reporting category.

11/19 09:21 Ministry of Education ↗

MOE names 41 camps, 20 bases and 20 provincial projects in lottery-funded study-trip review

At 09:21 on November 19 the finance and basic-education departments of the Ministry of Education published the review results for 2026 projects financed by the central special lottery public welfare fund for primary and secondary school study-travel activities. The round rests on the fund's management measures (Cai Jiao No. 142 of 2026); projects were proposed, vetted and recommended by provincial education departments before central review.

There are three lists. Forty-one camps, among them the Beijing Automation Engineering School, are in line for a service-quality upgrade. Twenty bases, including the Beijing Future Science and Technology Museum run by the Beijing Expo study-travel culture company, are in line for a programme that spreads bases more evenly across the map. And 20 provincial-level "red heritage" study-travel projects, submitted by provincial education departments including Beijing's, are also listed.

Comment runs from November 19 to 25. Objections must be in writing with supporting evidence and valid contact details; the numbers are 010-66093532, 010-66096369 and 010-66097531, the email cwjgc@moe.edu.cn, and the postal address is the budget division of the ministry's finance department, 37 Xidan Damucang Hutong, Beijing 100816. The three lists are PDF attachments; the notice gives no amount per project.

Why it mattersForty-one camps against 20 bases is the tell: twice as many camps, because whether children can stay overnight is the binding constraint. A county that wants to move a whole year group needs beds; without them the trip shrinks to a day and both the curriculum and the spend per student flatten. Splitting the money into quality upgrades for camps and coverage for bases concedes that earlier funding went into building sites rather than into receiving children. For schools the real cost is not the ticket: transport, insurance, teachers' time and lost lessons are absorbed locally, and whether a trip enters the teaching plan depends on whether it counts as an educational activity — and on who signs for safety.

Vocational & Careers

职教与就业
11/19 13:04 Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (via Southern Finance) ↗

Guangdong has built 168 industry-education skill chains, linking 1,100 employers and training 690,000

On November 19 the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security published a feature on Guangdong's industry-education skill chains, carried by Southern Finance. The totals: since the provincial human resources department started the programme in 2023, Guangdong has formed 168 chains, linked more than 1,100 companies and trained 690,000 workers.

The method is called the "three micros" — micro-programmes, micro-courses and micro-credentials — built by breaking a job into the smallest employable skill unit, with one post mapping to one or more credentials. Shein, as a chain leader, led the work on the garment quality inspector standard, which entered the national occupational classification in 2025; for people with no background it distilled 11 basic posts into standalone entry skills and refers those who pass to suppliers in the chain. Suppliers with Shein-certified inspectors saw defect rates fall by about 10%, the article says.

Nuclear power supplies the cost comparison. Yangjiang Nuclear Power and Yangjiang Technician College co-built a nuclear operations and maintenance industry college, turning valve assembly and pump servicing into units that can be taught and assessed in a classroom. Traditionally a new maintenance hire needed about three months of shadowing, at a direct training cost of roughly RMB 25,000; pre-training cuts that to about RMB 5,000. At 100 trainees a year the chain saves about RMB 2 million. More than 100 students finished the first dual-system and project cohorts in 2025, and all 97 in the 2026 cohort have signed internship contracts.

Assessment and competitions run alongside. The provincial human resources department backed 19 leading firms to host public skills contests this year, with winners eligible for senior-worker or technician certification. Guangshi Holdings has built standards from scratch for new roles such as embodied-AI robot application technician and ore processor, with nearly 2,000 staff expected to apply for certification in 2026; Siemens serves more than 10,000 manufacturers in the province.

Why it matters168 chains, 1,100 employers, 690,000 trained: the unit of organisation has changed, from schools hunting for partners to chain leaders defining standards, colleges teaching and the human resources department granting assessment rights. What shifts incentives is self-assessment and the "one test, two certificates" rule — a company will publish its internal standard only if it can issue the credential, and a college will rewrite its curriculum only if the credential counts. Costs move too: Yangjiang shifts RMB 25,000 per hire upstream into the college years, the RMB 2 million stays with employers, and the student's three years and tuition stay with the family. Replicability depends on having a chain leader; a county without one is back to building its own workshop.

11/19 16:04 Jinyun (Enorth) ↗

National machining-skills contest opens in Tianjin with teams from 17 provinces and three countries

On November 19 the machining-centre operator event of the third national equipment-manufacturing new-technology skills competition, part of the 2026 national industry skills competitions, opened at Tianjin Binhai Vocational College alongside the 2026 China-Russia skills invitational. Competitors come from China, Russia, Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan.

Teams come from 17 Chinese provinces plus the three countries. The organisers are the China Machinery Industry Education Association and the China Employment Training Technical Instruction Centre, with Tianjin Binhai Vocational College, the association's technical-education branch, and the human resources bureaux of the Tianjin Economic-Technological Development Area and the Binhai Hi-Tech Industrial Development Zone as co-hosts. Russia's Nakhodka State Humanities and Technology College and the Russia-China Education Association took part.

The event tests what a machining-centre operator actually does: complex process planning, precision CNC programming, coordination across operations and applied innovation. Its stated aims are to promote learning, training, assessment and programme building through competition, and to spread five-axis CNC technology into colleges and factories.

Why it mattersEmbedding a China-Russia invitational inside a national industry contest does two things at once: the domestic event tracks real five-axis practice, while foreign teams open a door to mutual recognition of standards. For a vocational college the return is not medals but machines and trainers — hosting an event brings five-axis tools, tooling and judges that are hard to budget for otherwise. For the district, hosting puts an advanced-manufacturing label on its vocational education. The limit is scope: one occupation, one event. Whether it becomes curriculum depends on whether anyone turns the tasks into teaching modules afterwards.

11/19 Hefei University of Technology, Academic Affairs Office ↗

MOE opens a second round of discipline LLMs: domestic base models, 100 concurrent users, local filing first

On November 19 the academic affairs offices of Hefei University of Technology and Southwest University, and the teaching affairs office of Jimei University's Chengyi College, forwarded the same education ministry notice opening a second round of applications for discipline-specific large models in higher education.

The notice sets two hard requirements. First, a high-quality dataset covering the relevant discipline group, drawn from textbooks, professional books, high-level papers, audio and video courses, syllabi, exercises and answers, terminology banks and industry standards, and spanning text, images, audio, video and code. Second, one discipline model, built by further training a domestic general-purpose model on that dataset, with an open service platform and interface supporting at least 100 concurrent users.

Eligibility and process: the university needs a research and application base in AI and large models; the discipline group must include at least one national first-class undergraduate programme; and the university must supply the computing power. Models must pass four evaluations — security, general disciplinary capability, discipline benchmarks and model efficiency — before going live. The path runs from recommendation to expert review to evaluation and filing to launch, with local cyberspace administration filing completed beforehand; approved models are listed on the national higher-education smart platform, which hosts a public-service zone for AI teaching as the single entry point. Southwest University caps its own submissions at five; Hefei wants materials by 12:00 on November 21.

Why it mattersThis turns the discipline model from a research project into teaching infrastructure. One of the four evaluations is efficiency, launch requires local cyberspace filing, and the entry point is a national platform — together that makes a model a service to be operated rather than a grant to be spent. The bar is concrete: a national first-class programme, own computing power, 100 concurrent users. The real cost is not one training run but dataset cleaning and annotation, plus compute, filing and re-evaluation afterwards. With places limited, universities will choose first, and the winners are usually faculties that already hold both a national programme and industry data. For teachers the model removes part of the lesson-planning and question-writing load; "disciplinarily accurate" still needs a human signature.

Youdao reports RMB 1.43 billion in Q3 revenue and a ninth straight profitable quarter

On November 19 NetEase Youdao (NYSE: DAO) reported unaudited third-quarter 2026 results. China Securities Journal reported at 19:12 that net revenue reached RMB 1.43 billion, that operating profit was RMB 30 million, up 19.2% year on year and positive for a ninth consecutive quarter, and that operating cash flow for the first nine months was a net inflow of RMB 150 million, a marked improvement on a year earlier. Against RMB 1.43 billion of revenue, RMB 30 million of operating profit implies a margin of about 2% (derived).

On the AI side the quarter was about open source and subscriptions. Youdao open-sourced two real-time interaction models in its Confucius Live series — the streaming speech-recognition model Confucius4-R2T2 and the streaming translation model Confucius4-T3PO — which topped Hugging Face's ASR and Translation trending lists respectively, with more than 200,000 downloads worldwide. Sina Tech reported the same day that AI subscription sales passed RMB 100 million, up more than 20%, that online marketing gross margin reached 29.6%, four points higher, and that advertising revenue from AI applications and short dramas grew more than 50% for a third consecutive quarter.

CEO Zhou Feng framed the quarter around the AI-native strategy: "In the third quarter we continued to execute our AI-native strategy, with operating efficiency and profitability both improving. Looking ahead, as our operations strengthen, AI technology advances and global partnerships deepen, we expect to meet the financial targets we set at the start of the year, with both operating profit and operating cash flow improving faster." The company also says its dictionary pen ranked first in its category on JD.com and Tmall for a seventh straight Double 11, that the office agent LobsterAI shipped version 2.0, and that the speaking-practice product Hi Echo became an agent-based tutor.

Why it mattersRMB 1.43 billion of revenue against RMB 30 million of operating profit is a 2% margin: nine profitable quarters in a row show Youdao can lose little rather than earn much. The only AI line disclosed separately is the RMB 100 million subscription business, about 7% of quarterly revenue (derived); at 20%-plus growth it would need five years or more to become a second curve. Two models topping Hugging Face and 200,000 downloads buy developer recognition, not revenue — what open source lowers is the barrier for business customers. For families and schools a subscription is a recurring monthly bill, easier to underestimate than a device bought once. The AI story in education comes down to who renews.
